# Power Systems — How Abilities Work in the World

⚠️ **Amended 2026-08-08 (SNG-376/377, Erik-ratified). The previous version opened "All power in this world
is nanite-mediated." That was the founding claim and it is no longer true — it was already partly
overturned by SNG-172 in July, and the cosmology below overturns the rest.**

## The Precursors

The Precursors are the entities Genesis describes — Father, Son, Holy Spirit, the angels. Very powerful
beings who harnessed enough power and technology to become effectively immortal and effectively
all-knowing. **They seeded life here. They built the systems that made the world habitable. Then they
disappeared.**

Everything they left is still running.

## The disagreement

What is beyond the Veil is not another species. **It is the same race.**

An ancient disagreement between two entities of one people, who followed different paths from it. What
came out the far end of that divergence, over spans a human lifetime cannot measure, is what the old
books call angels and demons. **Both sides began in the same place.**

⚠️ **This is not a war between good and evil, and the valley's own factions are not a moral scoreboard.**
It is the oldest argument there is, still running, conducted by beings who do not die and do not forget —
and it long ago stopped being about whatever it was originally about.

**Some people know all this and braid the two anyway, to great effect. They are not traitors. They are
people who declined to inherit an argument.**

## The four things power can be

**No single source explains everything.** A craft is powered by one or a mix.

1. **The Precursor lattice** — the built substrate that reads structured will and enacts it. Alien,
   ancient, still obeying instructions nobody living gave it. Strong where the lattice is dense.
2. **Nanite** — ⚠️ **separate from the lattice.** Machinery, deployed before the Transition, still
   running to its old specification. It goes where its makers put it and where people carry it, so it
   answers to *condition and supply*, not to ground. ⛔ **It is the one source with NO best-ground at
   all** — a nanite craft works the same on a mountain and in a lattice-dense hollow, and fails instead
   when the works are unmaintained or the supply is cut.
3. **Wild nanite** — the tangled, untended field of the Wild Half. It works, and it does not work the
   same way twice.
4. **Metaphysical** — mind reaching past matter, opened by the Transition. ⚠️ **It wants THIN ground:**
   a mind reaching past matter is interfered with by a dense lattice, not helped by it. It asks little of
   the place and everything of the practitioner. *(Corrected 2026-08-08: an earlier draft said it asks
   nothing of the ground, which contradicted the ratified school ruling — the Reaching Mind wants thin
   ground and the Instrumented wants dense, and that opposition is the whole point of schools.)*
5. **Body and technique** — trained flesh, breath, hand, and years. It asks nothing of anything.
   ⚠️ **Thin ground is quiet ground: natural craft is at its best where the lattice is at its worst.**

## The Veil

**The Veil is a defensive work.** It was put there on purpose, by the side that built everything else.

⛔ **Thinning it is not a technique for finding power. It is opening a breach.** The Numinous do it
anyway, for reasons they consider good. What comes through has its own reasons.

**The Veil is the anti-power to the lattice: it is thin where the lattice is weak, and thinning one
strengthens the other.** A craft drawn from beyond the Veil is powered by an absence, not a substance.

## What happens to the dead

A person is, among other things, a pattern — and a pattern is a thing that can be kept.

- **The lattice can save an identity within itself**, to be written onto a new body or entity later. What
  returns is a faithful record, restored. Continuity of pattern, not of flesh.
- **What is beyond the Veil can pull a presence back across.** Similar in outcome. Not similar in
  manner, and not similar in what has hold of it afterward.

⚠️ **This is why attending a death is work.** An unattended ending is an opportunity, and there are things
that watch for them. **Not every Ashwarden holds this reason — but the ones who do are not sentimental
about it, and they do not love the Abyssals.**

## Rules the narration must respect

1. **Distance matters, but not for everyone.** Lattice craft weakens away from dense ground. Natural
   craft is *better* on thin ground. Nanite craft follows its machinery and its people. ⚠️ **Every region
   is somebody's good ground.**
2. **Interference is real.** Where fields overlap, effects distort: sound bends light, light carries
   sound, small time-pockets form.
3. **Cost is real.** Every technique draws on something — energy, attention, condition, years. Nobody
   works all day.
4. **Skill is earned.** Initiate → Apprentice → Adept → Master → Grandmaster.
5. **Combination is potent and it is not treason.** Cross-source and cross-tradition braiding is rare,
   culturally fraught, and the likely key to the valley's mysteries. **Whoever tells you it is forbidden
   is telling you about themselves.**

## The two local craft families

- **Harmonic (sonic)** — resonance, echo-perception, standing-wave barriers, calming or shattering tones.
- **Radiant (photonic)** — light-bending, spectra-sight, coherent beams, mirage and revelation.

## The wider world (rumored, distant)

Beyond the mountains, five great civilizations shape whole climates with their own systems. To valley
folk these are traveler's tales. The mountain pass just made them real.
