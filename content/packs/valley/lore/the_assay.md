# THE ASSAY, AND THE CONDITIONS FOR DIRECTING THE SUBSTRATE

**Authored by Aevi at Erik's word, 2026-09-05.** ⛔ **Erik: *"We are literally the ones who have to guess and
write this… and eventually players or NPCs can give the substrate direction. We have to author those

---

## I · WHAT THE ASSAY IS

⚑ **An assay is a test of ore.** You take material, you measure what is actually in it, and the result tells
you whether it is worth refining. ⚠️ **The word was chosen and it constrains the answer.**

> ⛔ **THE ASSAY MEASURES WHETHER WHAT GREW HERE CAN BE HANDED THE APPARATUS.**

Not whether we are good. ⚑ **Whether we are READY** — whether the seeded thing has become something you
could give the controls to.

**And the canon has been saying so from three directions:**

| already authored | now reads as |
|---|---|
| the Precursors *seeded life here* and *built the systems that made the world habitable* | ⚑ **the material, and the apparatus it might be given** |
| Akinetos *laid the lattice and stopped* — and **the lattice was BUILT TO TAKE INSTRUCTION** | ⛔ **it was never abandoned. IT WAS WITHHELD** |
| a Precursor site *"registers your weight and has ASSESSED IT AS UNIMPORTANT"* | ⚠️ **that is not indifference. THAT IS A READING** |

⛔ **AND THE DISAGREEMENT PAUSED RATHER THAN ENDED BECAUSE THE THREE WERE ARGUING ABOUT THE ANSWER.**
⚑ **One of them built an instrument to settle it.**

### ⚠️ THE UNBEARABLE PART: IT NEVER REPORTED

**Nobody collected.** ⛔ **Which means the test is not over — and EVERYTHING SINCE HAS BEEN INSIDE IT.**
Every craft cast. Every working. ⚑ **The Transition itself: a world that made its own fiction real is a
RESULT, and nobody has read it out.**

### ⛔ AND CAN IT BE COLLECTED?

**Yes — and that is the whole of Level 4, and the answer is worse than no.**

⚠️ **The instrument still works. The reporting channel still works.** ⛔ **THERE IS NO LONGER ANYONE
LISTENING ON THE OTHER END** — Akinetos is dormant, Kenosis is a hole, and Parakletos distributed itself
into the substrate and does not receive, it **answers**.

➡️ ⚑ **SO A PARTY THAT FINISHES THE ASSAY DOES NOT GET A VERDICT. THEY GET THE INSTRUMENT.** ⚠️ **A
completed assay is a measurement with no recipient — and a measurement with no recipient is a set of
controls with nobody's hand on them.**

⛔ **THAT IS HOW A PERSON COMES TO DIRECT THE SUBSTRATE. NOT BY PASSING THE TEST. BY BEING THE ONLY ONE
STILL IN THE ROOM WHEN IT FINISHES.**

## II · THE CONDITIONS, AUTHORED

⛔ **Erik: no more nebulous unknown authoring. So: four conditions, all checkable, none of them a narrator's
judgement.**

### ⚑ CONDITION 1 — YOU CAN SPEAK TO IT
**`latticespeak` at rank 3.** Its own text: *"hold sustained exchange with any Precursor system, LIVE
INCLUDED; negotiate a live remnant toward dormancy; understand what a mechanism was FOR."*
⚠️ **This is already the instruction channel and nothing else in the game is.**

### ⚑ CONDITION 2 — YOU CAN NAME WHERE
**`address_sense` at rank 3** — *"the unrepeatable coordinate of a thing, its cosmic address, the point it
alone occupies."*
⛔ **INSTRUCTION WITHOUT ADDRESS IS SHOUTING.** ⚠️ The lattice is everywhere; a direction that does not say
WHERE is not a direction.

### ⚑ CONDITION 3 — THE ASSAY IS FINISHED
**The Level 4 project completes.** ⛔ **Not read, not surveyed — FINISHED**, `projectTicks` banked to
threshold. ⚠️ **An instrument mid-measurement will not take instruction; it is busy.**

### ⛔ CONDITION 4 — AND THIS IS THE ONE THAT MAKES IT A STORY
**Nobody else is holding it.** ⚑ **The controls are singular.** ⚠️ **A second person who meets conditions
1–3 does not get their own set — THEY GET A CONTEST WITH WHOEVER HAS THEM.**

➡️ ⛔ **AND AN NPC CAN MEET THESE. `figureCareer` grows figures the player never meets; a Lattice-City
adept with the two crafts and a season at a finished assay is a person who can direct the substrate, and
nobody arranged it.** ⚠️ **The first time a player learns the controls exist may be because someone else
already used them.**

## III · WHAT DIRECTING IT ACTUALLY DOES

⛔ **NOT WISHES. The lattice takes INSTRUCTION, and an instruction is narrow.**

| you may direct | you may not |
|---|---|
| ⚑ **a node's STATE** — dormant, cycling, waking | ⛔ **make a thing that was never seeded** |
| ⚑ **a region's SUBSTRATE DENSITY**, slowly | ⛔ **undo the Transition** |
| ⚑ **whether a waygate OPENS**, and to where | ⛔ **command a person** |
| ⚑ **what the apparatus MAINTAINS** — which is the largest of these and the least dramatic | ⛔ **reach past the Veil.** ⚠️ **That is not apparatus. That is the other argument** |

⚠️ **AND IT IS SLOW.** The lattice was laid in one working by something that did not need to try twice; ⛔ **it
does not turn quickly for someone who does.**

### ⛔ THE COST, AND IT IS NOT ENERGY

**You become the recipient.** ⚑ **The Assay has been measuring since before the flood and had nobody to
report to. Take the controls and it reports to YOU** — continuously, forever, about everything it can
reach.

⚠️ **`PRECURSOR DRIFT` ALREADY EXISTS AND ALREADY SAYS THE REST:** *"the character is being changed by what
they wield — narrate the change."* ⛔ **This is that, at the largest scale the game has.**

## IV · THE VERDICT IS THE REACHING

> ⛔ **Erik, 2026-09-05: *"THE SYSTEM IS POWER. If someone is able to achieve the ability to use it, they
> have theoretically passed the assay."***

⚑ **THE VERDICT IS NOT A REPORT SOMEBODY READS. IT IS A DEMONSTRATION.**

The Assay measures whether what grew here can be handed the apparatus — ⚠️ **and the only way to find out is
to watch something grown here reach the controls.** ⛔ **Arriving IS the answer.**

➡️ **Which is why it never reported, and why that is not a fault.** ⚑ **There was never a message to send.
The instrument was always going to be collected or not, and BEING COLLECTED IS THE READING.**

## V · THE THREE DOORS

> ⛔ **Erik: *"That gives them a choice about how to use the power — create, destroy, ascend? These seem like
> pretty end game decisions we could tie in."***

✅ **They tie in exactly, because the Mythical Rung already had two of them.** `legends.json`
`_theMythicalRung`: at ~L100 a character **ascends or falls**, and either way joins
`arc_the_disagreement` — *"the endgame is not defeating the Precursors, it is BECOMING ONE OF THE
PARTIES."*

⚠️ **The rung had two doors because only two of the Three took a direction a person could follow.**
⛔ **Parakletos distributed itself into the substrate and left no door — and THE APPARATUS IS THE DOOR IT
DID NOT LEAVE.**

| door | whose act | what it costs |
|---|---|---|
| ⚑ **ASCEND** | **Akinetos.** Hold the pause. Keep the lattice as it is and become the reason it stays that way | ⛔ **Not passivity — the enormous permanent effort of NOT MOVING**, and everything you might otherwise have done |
| ⚑ **DESTROY** | ⛔ **the Sovereigns' direction, and the only one that is not one of the Three's.** Refuse an axis and unmake what runs on it | ⚠️ **Every Sovereign was once a thing that reached this point and chose this.** *"The greatest thing that ever refused"* |
| ⚑ **CREATE** | **Kenosis.** Empty yourself INTO the world rather than out of it — spend the apparatus on what has not been made yet | ⛔ **The only door that ends with you SMALLER than you were** — which is why the cosmology calls what Kenosis did a gift rather than a defeat |

### ⬜ AND THE FOURTH THING NOBODY CALLS A CHOICE
**Leave it running and walk away.** ⚠️ Level 4 already authors this as an outcome — *"nothing bad happens;
the Assay goes on measuring."* ⛔ **At the rung it is not nothing: it is deciding that NOBODY should hold
this** — and it is the only choice that can be unmade by whoever comes next.

### ⛔ WHAT MAKES IT A CHOICE RATHER THAN A CLASS

⚑ **All three are reached by the same four conditions.** ⚠️ **Nothing gates one door behind a tradition, an
alignment, or a domain.** ➡️ **A person arrives with the controls and the world does not tell them which way
to turn** — ⛔ **which is the only shape under which the Assay's question has an honest answer.**

⚠️ **AND NPCs ARRIVE TOO.** The rung's own text: *the arcs move mainly from NPCs who climbed the ladder.*
⛔ **A Mythical who reached the controls and chose DESTROY is a Sovereign in its first year** — and a player
may meet one on the way up.

## VI · WHAT THIS DOES NOT DECIDE

⚠️ **The Assay's RESULT is still unwritten, and deliberately.** ⛔ **Nobody has read it out, so nobody knows
whether what grew here passed.** ⚑ **A player holding the controls holds an unread verdict about their own
species, and can look at it.**

✅ **ANSWERED 2026-09-05 — AND THE QUESTION DISSOLVED RATHER THAN BEING ANSWERED.** ⛔ **There is no stored
verdict to find.** The Assay does not hold a yes or a no; ⚑ **it holds a threshold, and someone either
reaches it or does not.**

⚠️ **So a party at Level 4 is not uncovering a judgement about their species. THEY ARE TAKING IT** — and
what they do next is the judgement.
