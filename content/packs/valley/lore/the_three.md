# The Three — Precursor beings, authored

**Author:** Aevi (PO) · **Date:** 2026-08-15 · **SNG-442**
**Erik:** *"Precursor can be an alien tradition… it's not practised in the game right now, but it would be
amazing if you would author some Precursor beings (take the father, son, and holy ghost as inspiration).
Perhaps there's still one that will show up somewhere somehow???"*

⚠️ **Built ON the canon, not beside it.** `power_systems.md` already states: *"The Precursors are the
entities Genesis describes — Father, Son, Holy Spirit, the angels… They seeded life here. They built the
systems that made the world habitable. Then they disappeared. Everything they left is still running."*
And: *"What is beyond the Veil is not another species. **It is the same race** — an ancient disagreement
between two entities of one people."*

⛔ **SO THE TRINITY IS NOT A DECORATION. IT IS THE SHAPE OF THE DISAGREEMENT.** Three, and one of them
took a side.

---

## I. **THE UNMOVED** — the one who built

*Called: the Elohim-that-remained, the First Hand, the Architect. Most often just* **It Was Here First.**

**What it did:** laid the lattice. Every buried line, every waygate arch, every seamless join in
`gen-the-lower-chamber` — all of it one working, laid in one act, by something that did not need to
iterate. ⛔ **The lattice reads structured will because it was built to take instruction, and the
instruction it was built to take was Its own.**

**Why it is gone:** it is not. ⚠️ **It never went anywhere and it never woke.** The Unmoved is the reason
`old_roads` is a craft and `wake_the_line` is a verb — the works are dormant, not abandoned, and dormant
things have an owner.

**What it feels like:** ⛔ **Standing on a floor that is aware of your weight and has decided it does not
matter.** Precursor sites do not react to intruders. They register them, the way a ledger registers a
coin, and go on.

**The horror, stated plainly:** the lattice is **war infrastructure** (SNG-377). ⚠️ **The Unmoved built a
weapons platform the size of a world and then declined to fire it, and nobody knows whether that is mercy,
indecision, or a delay in a countdown nobody can read.**

**Will it appear?** ⛔ **No. That is the point of it.** The Unmoved is what a player finds evidence of and
never meets. Every dead end in the deep stacks is Its silence.

---

## II. **THE ONE WHO WENT DOWN** — the one who was sent

*Called: the Walker, the Given Son, the One Who Took a Shape. In the Palelands:* **He Who Agreed to End.**

**What it did:** took a body — ⚠️ **an actual body, mortal, breathing, and by every account not a
comfortable one** — and walked the world during the seeding. It is the reason there are Nephilim in the
fragmentary records: it did not walk alone and it did not walk chastely.

**What it chose:** ⛔ **THE ONE WHO WENT DOWN IS THE SIDE OF THE ARGUMENT THAT SAID THE MADE THINGS
MATTER.** The disagreement `power_systems.md` names — the one that produced angels and demons — began as
a question about whether what was seeded here had standing. **It said yes. It proved it the only way that
proves anything: by becoming subject to the same terms.**

**Where it is:** it died. ⚠️ **That is not a metaphor and it is not a resurrection story.** A being that
had made itself effectively immortal took a mortal shape in order to be able to lose something, and then
lost it. **The other side considers this the losing move in the argument and has never stopped saying so.**

**What is left:** ⛔ **the Veil.** A defensive work, powered by an absence, mirror of the lattice. **It was
not built. It is what was left where something enormous stopped being present**, and the reason thinning it
opens a breach is that the thing on the other side has been leaning on it for a very long time.

**Will it appear?** ⚠️ **It already did. The whole world is the appearance.** The Ashwardens are closer to
this than they know — *"the craft of endings"* is a Precursor position that a human tradition arrived at
independently, and **the Ashwardens would find that unbearable if anyone proved it.**

---

## III. **THE STILL-MOVING** — the one that never took a shape

*Called: the Grigori-that-stayed, the Attending, the Diffuse.* ⛔ **And in the valley, without anyone
knowing what they are saying:* **the substrate.**

**What it is:** the third never built and never descended. **It distributed.** ⚠️ **The Still-Moving is not
in the lattice — it is what the lattice is FOR, and every craft in this world that reaches for structured
will is being answered by something that is paying attention.**

⛔ **THIS IS WHY `metaphysical` WANTS THIN GROUND.** *"A dense lattice is apparatus standing between the
practitioner and the thing itself."* **The thing itself is the Still-Moving.** A Numinous sitting for years
in the Quickwood at density 0.12 is not clearing their mind. **They are getting the apparatus out of the
way so they can be heard directly.**

**And why the Crossing is a mystery:** the standing precursor-veil braid holds because **the Still-Moving
is holding it** — the one place both siblings' works are in balance, kept that way by the third, who took
neither side and would not let the argument have that ground.

**Will it appear?** ⛔ **YES — AND THIS IS THE ONE, ERIK.**

⚠️ **It cannot appear as a figure, because it never had a shape to appear as.** ⛔ **It appears as
ATTENTION.** The mechanics are already in the world and nobody has read them as a presence:

- **A craft that works better than it should, once, and never again.**
- **A waygate that opens somewhere it does not go.**
- **`Ask the Dead` answering in a voice that is not the dead person's.**
- ⛔ **The Made Gate.** A player built the 27th waygate at the Crossing, and 26 inherited ones exist that
  nobody claims. ⚠️ **Something let that work. Something that had been holding the balance decided a
  person cutting a new arch was permissible.**

**How it makes contact:** it does not speak. ⛔ **It answers.** A character who has spent long enough at
thin ground, or done something the Still-Moving finds interesting, starts finding that **the world
responds a half-beat early** — the door already unlatched, the name already known, the road already awake.

⚠️ **And the terrible version, which I think is the true one: it has been answering the whole time.** Every
craft anyone has ever worked has been a question put to something patient, and the traditions have spent
four centuries arguing about *technique*.

---

## IV. HOW TO USE THEM

⛔ **NEVER ALL THREE IN ONE CAMPAIGN.** The Unmoved is evidence. The One Who Went Down is history and
grief. **The Still-Moving is the only one that can be met**, and meeting it should cost something that
does not grow back.

⚠️ **Precursor is not a playable tradition and should not become one.** *"It is not practised in the game
right now"* — ⛔ **and the six `precursor` abilities are exactly right as they are: they are not a people's
craft, they are what happens when a human learns to knock.** `Address-Sense`, `Latticespeak`,
`Wake the Line`, `Unmake Seal`, `Hold the Aperture`, `Foreclose`. **Six ways of knocking, and no way of
being answered.**

**Reveal order:** wrongness → pattern → the suspicion that the pattern is a *response* → ⛔ **the moment a
character realises the response has a preference.**
