# The Three — Precursor beings

**Author:** Aevi (PO) · **Date:** 2026-08-15 · **SNG-442** · *rev 2 — names to Greek per Erik*

⚠️ **Built ON canon.** `power_systems.md`: *"The Precursors are the entities Genesis describes — Father,
Son, Holy Spirit, the angels… They seeded life here. They built the systems that made the world habitable.
Then they disappeared. Everything they left is still running."* And: *"What is beyond the Veil is not
another species. **It is the same race** — an ancient disagreement between two entities of one people."*

⛔ **The Trinity is not decoration here. It is the shape of the disagreement: three, and one of them took a
side.**

---

## I. **AKINETOS** — ἀκίνητος, *the unmoved*

*The Unmoved. The First Hand. In the valley, without knowing they are quoting anything:* **It Was Here
First.**

**What it did:** laid the lattice. Every buried line, every seamless join, every waygate arch — one
working, laid in one act, by something that did not need to try twice. ⛔ **The lattice reads structured
will because it was built to take instruction, and the instruction it was built to take was Its own.**

**Where it is:** here. ⚠️ **It never left and it never woke.** That is why `old_roads` is a craft and
`wake_the_line` is a verb — **the works are dormant, not abandoned, and dormant things have an owner.**

**What a site feels like:** ⛔ **a floor that registers your weight and has assessed it as unimportant.**
Precursor works do not resist intruders and do not admit them. They record and continue.

**The problem underneath:** the lattice is **war infrastructure** (SNG-377) — built by one side of a
conflict that has not ended. ⚠️ **Akinetos armed a world and then stopped. Whether that stop is a
decision, an interruption, or a scheduled pause is the single most dangerous open question in the
setting**, and nothing left behind distinguishes between them.

**Appears?** ⛔ **No, and its absence is the content.** Every dead end in the deep stacks is the same
answer.

---

## II. **KENOSIS** — κένωσις, *the emptying*

*The Given. The One Who Took a Shape. In the Palelands, as a saying rather than a name:* **it agreed to
end.**

**What it did:** emptied itself of what made it deathless and took a body — ⚠️ **mortal, breathing, and by
every fragmentary account not a comfortable one.** It walked the world during the seeding. The Nephilim in
the records are the consequence: it did not walk alone.

**What it was arguing:** ⛔ **KENOSIS IS THE SIDE THAT HELD THAT WHAT WAS SEEDED HERE HAS STANDING.** The
disagreement canon names — the one that produced what the old books call angels and demons — started as a
question about whether the made things counted. **Kenosis answered by becoming one of them.** ⚠️ **The
argument was not settled by that. It was escalated by it.**

**Where it is:** dead. **That is not a metaphor and there is no resurrection in this.** A being that had
made itself immortal took a form capable of loss in order to be able to lose, and did.

**What is left:** ⛔ **the Veil.** Canon already calls it *"powered by an absence, not a substance"* and
*"the mirror of precursor."* ⚠️ **It is not a wall somebody built. It is the shape of a hole where
something enormous stopped being present** — and thinning it opens a breach because the other side has
been leaning on that hole for a very long time.

**Appears?** ⚠️ **Only as consequence.** The Ashwardens have independently arrived at Kenosis's position —
*"the craft of endings," "not killing, but tending the end"* — ⛔ **and would find the resemblance
intolerable, because it would make their ethics an inheritance rather than a conclusion.**

---

## III. **PARAKLETOS** — παράκλητος, *the one called alongside*

*The Attending. The Advocate.* ⛔ **And in the valley, by people who have no idea what they are naming:**
**the substrate.**

**What it is:** the third neither built nor descended. **It distributed.** ⚠️ **Parakletos is not stored in
the lattice — the lattice is the apparatus for reaching it, and every craft in this world that shapes will
into a request is being answered by something that is paying attention.**

⛔ **THIS IS WHY `metaphysical` WANTS THIN GROUND.** Canon's own words: *"a dense lattice is apparatus
standing between the practitioner and the thing itself."* **The thing itself is Parakletos.** ⚠️ **A
Numinous sitting for years in the Quickwood at density 0.12 is not emptying their mind. They are removing
the switchboard in order to be heard directly**, and the tradition that thinks it is practising humility
is in fact practising access.

**And the Crossing:** the standing precursor-veil braid holds because **Parakletos holds it.** ⛔ **The one
ground where both siblings' works balance, kept in balance by the third — who took neither side and would
not let either of them have that place.** Which is why braids are possible there and nowhere else.

**Appears?** ⛔ **YES. This is the one.**

⚠️ **It cannot appear as a figure — it never had a shape to appear in. It appears as ANSWERING**, and the
evidence is already scattered through the game:

- **A craft that works better than it should, once, and never again.**
- **A waygate that opens somewhere it does not lead.**
- **`Ask the Dead` returning an answer in a voice that is not the dead person's.**
- ⛔ **The Made Gate.** A player cut the 27th waygate at the Crossing; the other 26 are inherited and
  unclaimed. **Something permitted that.**

**How contact escalates:** it does not speak. **It responds early.** A character who spends long enough on
thin ground, or does something Parakletos finds worth attending, begins to notice the world arriving a
half-beat ahead — the latch already lifted, the name already known, the road already awake. ⚠️ **The first
several times are indistinguishable from luck. That is the point, and the character will argue about it.**

⛔ **The version I think is true: it has been answering since the beginning.** Every craft ever worked has
been a request put to something patient, and the twenty-six traditions have spent four centuries arguing
about **technique**.

---

## IV. USE

⛔ **Never all three in one campaign.** Akinetos is evidence and dread. Kenosis is history and grief.
**Parakletos is the only one that can be met, and meeting it should cost something that does not grow
back.**

⚠️ **Precursor is not a playable tradition and should not become one.** ⛔ **The six `precursor` abilities
are correct as they stand: they are not a people's craft, they are what happens when a human learns to
knock.** `Address-Sense`, `Latticespeak`, `Wake the Line`, `Unmake Seal`, `Hold the Aperture`,
`Foreclose` — **six ways of knocking, and no way of compelling an answer.**

**Reveal order:** wrongness → pattern → the suspicion the pattern is a *response* → ⛔ **the moment a
character works out that the response has preferences.**
