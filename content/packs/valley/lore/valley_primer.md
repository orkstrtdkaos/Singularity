# The Valley of Echoes — a regional primer

*One region of Exesa, and the argument it is having. For the world itself, read* Exesa — the world, entire.

## Where this sits

The Valley of Echoes is a fertile mountain valley holding ten of the world's hundred and thirty-seven
places. It is not the world and it is not a summary of the world — but it is the argument in miniature, which
is why people who have never been here have opinions about it.

## What happened before

Humanity reached technological parity with the Precursors, and most of the world then stepped back from it —
a voluntary de-technologising. Not collapse. Renunciation. What remains is a world where nanite-integrated
power shaped by old philosophies stands where industrial machinery used to, and where the question *how much
of what we built should we keep?* divides every community that has to answer it.

In the Valley they call that the Great Transition and date it to fifteen years ago. Elsewhere it has other
names and other dates, because it did not happen everywhere at once.

## The Valley

- **Millbrook and the valley floor** — unaligned farming communities, and three loose factions among them:
  the Traditionalists, who would keep the old technology buried; the Adaptors, who want selective and
  sustainable use of it; and the Explorers, who are curious and outward-looking and are distrusted by both of
  the others.
- **The Harmonic Heights** — a terraced sonic civilization. Quantum-acoustic craft: buildings that resonate,
  weather tuned by great chimes, messages carried as tone. The culture prizes patience, tradition and
  consonance. Their power fades with distance from the resonance matrix.
- **The Radiant Plateau** — a photonic civilization on the opposite rim. Light-guide streets, optical
  computing, mirage-craft. The culture prizes clarity, revelation and precision, while being masters of
  making you see what they choose. Their power fades with distance from the photonic array.
- **The Disputed Zone** — the interference band between the two power fields, where sound bends light and
  light carries sound. Lawless, strange, and littered with pre-Transition remains.
- **Archive Hollow** — a recently exposed pre-Transition knowledge vault, and the valley's most dangerous
  political object.

## What is live here

1. **The water.** The Echo River shows spreading contamination, and each civilization suspects the other.
   The truth is worse than either suspicion, and it is not local.
2. **The Archive.** Preserve it, study it, or seal it — every faction wants a different answer, and the
   question is not really about the Archive.
3. **The way out.** A route out of the valley has opened for the first time in living memory. There are
   fresh boot prints on the far side, and nobody knows whose.

## Tone

Grounded hopeful-strange. Communities genuinely trying to cooperate under strain. Wonder and dread in equal
measure at the edges — the Precursor layer should feel old and vast, glimpsed, never explained all at once.
Violence is possible, and always costly, and always has consequences.
