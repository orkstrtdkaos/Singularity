# ARCHETYPES BY DOMAIN — who you would be, and what you would take

**Companion to `SKILLS.md`.** ⛔ **That file is GENERATED and lists every craft. This one is HAND-AUTHORED and
says what a person actually builds.** ⚠️ Where a craft id here does not exist in `SKILLS.md`, **it is a
hole and it is marked as one.**

**Erik, 2026-09-08:** *"What types of heroes or champions or adventurers would be common to find in each?
What does what they do tell us about what skills they would pick?"*

⚑ **THE METHOD:** write the kit a player would actually reach for, then check every id against the
catalogue. ⛔ **A miss is either a hole or a wrong guess, and telling those apart is the whole value.**

---

# DEATH — 44 crafts · ashwarden 24 · threnodist 20

⚑ **Ashwarden attends what ends. Threnodist carries what it costs.** ⚠️ **Every archetype here is a
different answer to *"someone died, what now?"***

### THE WARDEN — *the attending road*
`deathsense` · `the_attended_end` · `kept_vigil` · `palework` → `grey_road` · `kept_breath` · `soul_stare`
→ `death_ward` · `ask_the_dead` · `grey_ground` → `deathless`

⛑ **The whole kit is `foresee`, `reveal`, `ward`, `sustain` — a warden's crafts are about STAYING, not
stopping.** ⚠️ **`hastened_grey` sits at T1 and most wardens never take it. THE REFUSAL IS THE CLASS**, and
`closed: ["hastened_grey"]` is authored on Siol, Ama and Oreth for exactly that reason.

### ⛔ THE GRAVE-CALLER — *and this is a hole*
`set_hand` (T3) · `given_errand` (T4) · `calling_back` (T3)

⛔ **THREE CRAFTS AND NO LADDER.** `set_hand` is authored as *"⚠️ RAISING — the Grave-Callers' craft, and the
Palelands have never pretended otherwise."* ⚠️ **A grave-caller cannot start small and cannot get better:
they arrive at T3 fully formed and stop.** ⛑ **And `narrowed_dead` is an authored bestiary class that
nothing a player casts can produce.**

### THE THRENODIST — *grief as a working*
`carried_name` · `shared_weight` · `storykeeper` · `the_true_feeling` · `wellspring` → `pathos` ·
`harmonic_voice` · `made_elegy` → `echo_memory` · `names_of_the_lost` · `worldsong`

⚑ **The first four contain NO HARM AT ALL** — `restore`, `shield`, `reveal`, `sustain`. **The pole's violence
is entirely T2+.**

### THE MOURNER-KILLER — *the sharp read of the same pole*
`keening` · `grief_strike` · `last_cold` · `wither` · `dread` → `grief_that_stops` (T5, lethal)

⚠️ **Same domain, and it is a killer's list** — because Pathos INSTALLS a feeling and `grief_that_stops`
installs one *"at a weight no body holds."*

### THE UNDYING
`deathless` · `kept_breath` · `draw_down` · `shared_weight` — ⚑ **a whole build around not stopping.**

⬜ **Domain note:** ⚠️ **Death has no `make` craft but `made_elegy` and no `travel` but `grey_road`.** ⛑ It
reveals, wards, sustains and harms — **and builds almost nothing.** That is probably correct for it.

# BUILDING — 33 crafts · wright 20 · stillhold 13

### THE ARTIFICER — *complete, 12/12*
`makers_eye` · `tinkers_hand` · `raised_thing` · `held_repair` · `quick_work` → `makecraft` ·
`weapon_at_hand` · `ongoing_work` → `true_making` → `reforging` → `masterwork`

⚑ **The cleanest ladder in the game: five T1s, and every rung visibly better at the same thing.** ⚠️
`reforging` is the one to notice — *"where it broke is now where it is strongest"* — **a T4 that is a
philosophy.**

### THE LIGHTWRIGHT — *a wright who works in light rather than matter*
`glasswork` · `light_well` · `glimmer_script` → `clarity_lens` → `sun_seal`

⛑ **Five crafts and it is a real specialisation** — ⚠️ **and it shows that Wright is not one path but two.**

### THE PEACEMAKER — *the Stillhold's whole argument*
`read_the_room` · `calm_word` · `mediators_tongue` · `broken_quiet` → `price_shown` · `step_between` ·
`safe_ground` → `broker_truce` → `held_breath` · `quieting`

⛔ **NOT A PACIFIST BUILD.** ⚠️ `step_between` puts you in the way, `held_breath` and `quieting` are T4 —
⛑ **holding a peace is expensive and the ladder says so.**

### THE HEARTH-KEEPER
`hearthbinding` · `laid_ground` · `safe_ground` · `stillcraft` · `held_repair`

⚑ **The holdings build.** ⚠️ **Every one of these makes a PLACE hold**, which is why Ama and the Unbought
Court both draw here.

### ⛔ THE RULER — *the hole, and it is the biggest one found*
**Wants:** ⛔ `the_gathering` · `raise_banner` · `lead_the_line` · `command_field` — ⚠️ **NONE EXIST.**

**MEASURED: 30 crafts carry `command` and every one commands A PERSON, A MACHINE OR A SPIRIT.** `calm_word`
talks one person down; `blazing_word` holds a room; `command_engine` directs a mechanism. ⛔ **NOTHING
COMMANDS A BAND.**

⚑ **And the engine is already waiting** — the standing ladder, `delegationCapacity`, `bandOps`, the
garrison, R46a's `legionClash`. ⚠️ **A player becomes a leader by ACCUMULATING STANDING and can never become
one by BEING GOOD AT IT.**
