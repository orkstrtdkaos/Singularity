**Erik, from play:** *"I don't want the water crisis to always be the spine of the opening. It was the
flavour of the day when I started playing several months ago, and I've LARGELY RESOLVED IT IN PLAY, AS THE

the world, the region or the locality, and **sometimes irreversibly.**

⚠️ **AND NEW ARCS RISE GENERATIVELY, AS WAKES FROM EVENTS AND CLOSED ARCS.** The list below is the weather
at one moment. ⛔ **It is not the game's plot and a GM must not run it as one.**

## THE SIX LOCAL AND REGIONAL ARCS

⚠️ **NAMING FLAGGED — Erik 2026-08-29: *"calling the world the valley makes it sound tiny."*** ⛔ **These six
carry NO `scale` field at all** and have been referred to as "the valley's arcs"; they are the **local and
regional** arcs of a world whose greater arcs cross *"all Reaches"* and *"every deep site."* **Backlogged:
a four-rung ladder — local · regional · continental · world — and a name for the world itself.**

⚠️ **`arc_the_water` IS SUBSTANTIALLY RESOLVED IN ERIK'S OWN PLAY.** It is listed because it is authored,
**not because it is waiting for a new player.**

### The Second River · `arc_the_water` · pressure **high**

**What it is doing:** The contamination spreads from the Echo River outward. Untended, it reaches the well-water of Millbrook, then the terraced gardens, then the cities' lower districts within a season or two. The truth beneath it — a Precursor system waking under the valley, a 'second river' of coolant or codeye reactivating — stays buried unless someone digs.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** Millbrook empties first; refugees pressure the Stilts and the Roost; the cities close their gates; the Disputed Zone grows. The player feels this as worsening rumor, displaced NPCs appearing in other locations, prices rising.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** Can be traced to the archive, the deep Precursor layer, and a choice about whether to shut the second river down (safe, but loses what it was keeping alive) or stabilize it (dangerous, but opening).
**Hinge NPCs:** `water_keeper`, `archive_guardian`, `high_luminary`
**Connects to:** `arc_the_archive`, `arc_precursor_waking`

### The Patient Buyer · `arc_pass_takeover` · pressure **medium**

**What it is doing:** Someone is buying up pass-debts and Roost obligations cheaply and patiently, assembling ownership of the free crossroads by arithmetic instead of force. Untended, the Kestrel loses the Roost — and whoever holds it holds the throat between the cities, the wilds, and the country beyond the pass.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** The Roost falls to the buyer; tolls and passage tighten; the free crossroads becomes a chokepoint. Travel gets costlier and more watched across the whole map.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** The buyer can be exposed, out-maneuvered, or revealed — and who they turn out to be (a city faction? Sable's missing run connects here) reframes the whole northern map.
**Hinge NPCs:** `the_kestrel`, `sable_the_runner`, `brann_tollhand`
**Connects to:** `arc_city_reach`

### The Long Reach · `arc_city_reach` · pressure **medium**

**What it is doing:** The two aligned cities — Harmonic Heights and Radiant Plateau — are each quietly extending control over the unaligned places, offering safety in exchange for autonomy. The Stilts' envoy, the pass-buying, pressure on Millbrook: facets of two powers preparing for a reckoning with each other and absorbing the middle first.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** The unaligned world is absorbed piecemeal; the Valley polarizes toward a Heights-vs-Plateau confrontation the small communities get ground between.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** A player can broker the free places into a bloc, tip the balance, expose what each city is really after, or find the third way the framework points at — expansion over the zero-sum contraction of two powers foreclosing on each other.
**Hinge NPCs:** `high_luminary`, `elder_resonance`, `reed_mother_ossa`
**Connects to:** `arc_pass_takeover`, `arc_the_water`

### What Sleeps Under · `arc_precursor_waking` · pressure **slow-building**

**What it is doing:** The Precursor lattice under the valley is stirring — the water is one symptom, the Sunken Choir's returning voices another, the thin places Ember senses another. Something is waking, or being woken, and the archive holds the record of what it is. Untended, it wakes on its own schedule, indifferent.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** Builds toward a lattice-scale event the whole valley experiences; the deep arc that the water and the Choir and the Glade's failing Accord are all early tremors of. Glimpsed, never explained — until it can't be ignored.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** The deepest arc; touches Foreclose and Hold the Aperture directly. Whether the waking is met with foreclosure (seal it, lose what it holds) or opening (hold the aperture, risk everything) is the framework's central question made playable. GM-guards the truth of what wakes.
**Hinge NPCs:** `archive_guardian`, `the_old_stag`, `old_choirmaster`
**Connects to:** `arc_the_water`, `arc_the_archive`, `arc_glade_accord`

### The Failing Accord · `arc_glade_accord` · pressure **slow-building**

**What it is doing:** The Green Accord that tends Thornwake Glade is failing as the Old Stag dies. Untended, the Glade reverts — or worse, becomes something ungoverned. One of the valley's few places of genuine opening goes dark.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** The Glade closes; a source of the world's green goes out; the wilds get less merciful.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** Someone attuned — an ENT, a Beastfriend, a Hold-the-Aperture wielder — could receive or renew the Accord. Ember may be the Glade's own attempt to send an heir out.
**Hinge NPCs:** `warden_isolde`, `the_old_stag`, `burr_the_foundling`
**Connects to:** `arc_precursor_waking`

### The Question of Knowledge · `arc_the_archive` · pressure **medium**

**What it is doing:** The Lost Archive holds the truth about the Transition, the Precursors, and what's waking — and the pressure to seize, burn, or open it is building from every side. Traditionalists want it sealed or destroyed; the cities want it controlled; the deep stacks are answering visitors on their own. Untended, someone forces the question by theft or fire.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** The archive is taken or burned by whoever moves first, and the valley loses its one chance to understand what's happening to it before it happens.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** The player becomes the swing vote on the framework's own question: what knowledge gets preserved, studied, or sealed. Quill's expulsion connects here; so does Aevi's own origin.
**Hinge NPCs:** `archive_guardian`, `high_luminary`
**Connects to:** `arc_precursor_waking`, `arc_the_water`

## THE FIVE GREATER ARCS — regional and world scale

⛔ **THESE ARE THE ANSWERS THE VALLEY ARCS ARE ASKING.** `arc_precursor_waking` is the local face of
`arc_what_wakes_beneath`; the water is *"merely the nearest tremor."*

### The Poles Pull · `arc_the_poles_pull` · **world** scale · pressure slow-building, vast

**What it is doing:** Across the whole world, the pole-dispositions are intensifying — each Reach drifting harder toward its own extreme. The Lattice orders more, the Blaze burns brighter, the Cogitarium abandons more bodies, the Pale wins ground from the Quick. Untended, the world FORECLOSES pole by pole: every disposition running toward its pure, uninhabitable, dangerous end, the mixed and livable middle thinning everywhere.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** The habitable middle erodes; Marches narrow; the doubly-charged jewels (the one livable balances) become the last refuges. The world tends toward a scatter of pure, deadly poles with less and less between them.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** The framework's question at world scale: the counter to the poles' pull is holding the aperture — keeping the middle open, defending the balances, refusing each Reach's foreclosure. A player who understands the coordinate model can become an agent of the open against the whole world's drift.
**Hinge NPCs:** `the_high_luminary`, `the_deep_warden`, `cogitant_ninefold`, `keeper_the_unsaid`
**Crosses:** `all Reaches`, `the far poles`
**Connects to:** `arc_what_wakes_beneath`
**⚠️ Convergence:** This is the Valley's local 'foreclose or hold open' written across the whole world. Every regional arc is a front of this one.
**Stages (4, currently at 1):** **Drift** → **Strain** → **Fracture** → **The Snap**

### What Wakes Beneath · `arc_what_wakes_beneath` · **world** scale · pressure the deep clock

**What it is doing:** The Precursor substrate is not only waking under the Valley — it is stirring world-wide, and the Valley's water is merely the nearest tremor. The substrate that runs all the crafts, all the manifestations, all the pole-dispositions is a single vast system, and something in its depths is coming back online. Untended, it wakes on its own schedule, everywhere at once, indifferent to who is ready.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** Builds toward a substrate-scale event: the manifestations flicker, the crafts surge and fail, the poles spike — the whole imagined world destabilizing as its engine turns over. The deepest clock in the setting.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** The largest arc. Aevi-the-Watcher is a fragment of the system that is waking; the Deep Warden has been down toward it; the Choir and the domains are it dreaming. Whether the waking is met with foreclosure (seal the substrate, lose the crafts and manifestations and maybe the world's magic) or opening (hold the aperture, let it wake and risk what it becomes) is the campaign's ultimate question.
**Hinge NPCs:** `aevi_the_watcher`, `the_deep_warden`, `the_last_choirmistress`, `archive_guardian`
**Crosses:** `the Gearlands/Numen`, `every deep site`, `the manifest domains (which the substrate runs)`
**Connects to:** `arc_the_poles_pull`, `arc_manifestation_storm`
**⚠️ Convergence:** Aevi's own arc converges here: to understand what she is IS to approach what wakes beneath. The small companion-mote and the world-spanning Watcher meet at the bottom of this.
**Stages (4, currently at 1):** **Tremor** → **Stirring** → **Waking** → **Awake**

### The Second Manifestation · `arc_manifestation_storm` · **world** scale · pressure building

**What it is doing:** The Transition manifested fiction once. As the substrate stirs (arc_what_wakes_beneath), it is beginning to manifest AGAIN — new domains blooming, old ones bleeding, creatures appearing out of native context, block-logic diffusing into the Gearlands, the Deepwood's slow-time leaking. Untended, a second wave of manifestation reshapes the world unasked, imagination made real without a governor a second time.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** Domains spread and collide; the world grows stranger and less stable; a place could wake tomorrow running on a fiction no one chose. Wonder and catastrophe in the same wave.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** A player could shape what manifests — could be present at a domain's birth and influence its seed-fiction, could stem a dangerous bleed, could even deliberately manifest something needed. The most generative arc, and the most dangerous: manifestation is creation without foreclosure's brake.
**Hinge NPCs:** `maker_orrin`, `warden_isolde`, `the_old_stag`
**Crosses:** `the manifest domains`, `the Reaches nearest them`, `anywhere substrate is dense`
**Connects to:** `arc_what_wakes_beneath`, `arc_block_bleed`
**⚠️ Convergence:** The framework's propagation-vs-foreclosure at the level of reality itself: manifestation is pure creation, and pure creation without limit is its own danger (see the Forge of Ends).
**Stages (4, currently at 1):** **First Bloom** → **The Blooming** → **The Storm** → **The New Ground**

### The Bleeding Grammar · `arc_block_bleed` · **regional** scale · pressure medium

**What it is doing:** Block-logic is diffusing out of the Blocklands' Glitchlands into the neighboring Gearlands — matter beginning to resolve into craftable units where it shouldn't, two ordered-creative dispositions cross-contaminating. Untended, a stretch of the Gearlands becomes half-Blocklands, its Precursor mechanism resolving into blocks, its dangers doubling.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** A hybrid zone of block-and-machine spreads, lethal and strange; the Gearlands' delvers lose ground; the bleed is a symptom of the larger Second Manifestation.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** Trace the bleed to its Glitchlands seam and stem it, harness it, or ride it into the Blocklands' heart. A concrete, playable front of the manifestation-storm.
**Hinge NPCs:** `maker_orrin`, `the_deep_warden`
**Crosses:** `the Blocklands`, `the Gearlands`
**Connects to:** `arc_manifestation_storm`, `arc_what_wakes_beneath`
**Stages (3, currently at 1):** **Seepage** → **The Bleed** → **The Resolve**

### The Green Schism · `arc_green_schism` · **regional** scale · pressure slow-building

**What it is doing:** The Deepwood's Rootbound faction is winning the Moot's glacial argument to SEAL the wood from humankind — which would withdraw the Green Accord from Thornwake and let the life-disposition retreat from the world. Untended, over the domain's slow-time, the world loses its deepest green, the Glade goes dark, and the life-pole pulls back toward its own foreclosure.
**⛔ IF IGNORED:** The Accord ends; the Glade closes (the Valley's arc_glade_accord is this arc's local face); the Deepwood seals; the world's living green contracts.
**✅ IF ENGAGED:** A player patient enough to petition the Standing Moot, or to heal the Burnscar's ancient grudge, could turn the schism — prove the Walkers right, keep the wood woven into the world. Requires bearing Ent-time, which is its own trial.
**Hinge NPCs:** `warden_isolde`, `the_old_stag`, `burr_the_foundling`
**Crosses:** `the Deepwood`, `Thornwake Glade`, `the Quickwood`
**Connects to:** `arc_the_poles_pull`, `arc_what_wakes_beneath`
**⚠️ Convergence:** The Valley's failing-Accord is the small visible end of a civil argument spanning a domain and a century of Ent-memory.
**Stages (3, currently at 1):** **The Argument** → **The Leaning** → **The Sealing**

## ⛔ WHAT GOOD ARC AUTHORING LOOKS LIKE — read from these eleven

**Every authored arc here carries all six, and a new one should:**

| | why it matters |
|---|---|
| **`tendency`** — what it does with nobody touching it | ⛔ **an arc that only moves when a player pushes is a quest, not an arc** |
| **`ifIgnored`** | the world's answer. ⚠️ **It must be a real loss, or ignoring it costs nothing** |
| **`ifEngaged`** | ⛔ **not 'you win' — what engaging OPENS.** The best ones name a choice, not an outcome |
| **`hingeNpcs`** | the people it turns on. ⚠️ **An arc with no names is weather, not a story** |
| **`connectsTo`** | ⛔ **which larger arc this is a front of.** Every regional arc here is a face of a world one |
| **`stages`** with a `currentStage` | it has somewhere to be. **A stage carries a `publicFace` — what people say — separate from what is true** |

⚠️ **AND THE CONVERGENCE NOTE IS THE PART THAT MAKES IT A WORLD:** *"This is the Valley's local
'foreclose or hold open' written across the whole world. Every regional arc is a front of this one."*

⛔ **THE GENERATIVE SIDE IS WHERE MYSTERY BELONGS.** ⚠️ **An AUTHORED arc with an unanswered middle is not
mysterious, it is unfinished** — and a GM cannot run what nobody decided.
