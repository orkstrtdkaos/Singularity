### The Silent Door

**hiddenTruth** *(chronicle_played_gambits.json)*

The door was not a lock but a VAULT, and the 'toll' was a deposit-to-reclaim, not a sacrifice. Teva's lost knowledge was held inside, intact — she was an account-holder in arrears, not locked out. ### the_living_world

### Inscription (rune-work)

**gmGuidance** *(will_modality.json)*

Let any ability be inscribed rather than cast for a time+durability tradeoff. A Vivimancer can INSCRIBE Quickening into a field to bloom for a season; an Enginewright inscribes a command that runs when they're gone; a rune-caster inscribes a reading-ward that flags what crosses it. The rune's STRUCTURE encodes the ability + its parameters.

### skill_utility_audit

**gmMandate** *(skill_utility_audit.json)*
