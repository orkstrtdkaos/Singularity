## HOW TO READ A FACTOR

`bandFactor` returns a **multiplier**, not a rate: **1.0** inside the band, `pow(x, starveExp)` below it,
`1 − crowdSlope·over` above it. ⚠️ **So 1.00 means "this ground is as good as it gets for this source", and
0.20 means the ground is fighting you.**

⬜ **A base success PERCENTAGE is a different number and it is not in this atlas** — it needs a stated
reference contest (`SPEC_ground_card_everywhere` §2, §6 Q1), and a percentage printed without its reference
is the kind of figure nobody can argue with.
