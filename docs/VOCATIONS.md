# THE VOCATIONS — what a person is FOR, and how each one survives

**Companion to `SKILLS.md`.** ⛔ **That file is GENERATED and lists every craft. This one is HAND-AUTHORED.**
⚠️ **Read this first and `ARCHETYPES.md` second: THE VOCATIONS ARE THE CLASSES; the domain entries are how
each one LOOKS in a given pole.**

---

## §1 — ⛑ THE VOCATIONS ARE NOT THE FUNCTION FAMILIES

⛔ **Tested, and the test failed usefully.** ⚠️ **Clustering thirty domain-expressions by their dominant
function family put TWELVE OF THIRTY in PROTECT, one in SUSTAIN, and never rose above 52% for any class.**

⚑ **THAT IS NOT A TAXONOMY, IT IS NOISE — and the reason is clean:** ⛔ **the eight families are MECHANICAL —
what a craft does in a round. A vocation is what a person is FOR.** ⚠️ **A Paladin and a Peacemaker are both
PROTECT-dominant and they are not remotely the same job.**

---

## §2 — ⚑ THE EIGHT, AND WHAT EACH ONE DOES WHEN A FIGHT STARTS

> **Erik: *"In almost every RPG you need to be able to fight — and it's fun to do so. But in this game I want
> ALL APPROACHES TO BE VIABLE IN THE MAJORITY OF SITUATIONS."***

⛑ **AND THE GAME ALREADY BUILT THE PROOF: the coliseum is EIGHT CELLS, ONE PER FAMILY, and each one exists
to demonstrate that a different way of being wins a fight.** ⚠️ **Every vocation below names its cell — the
authored champion who fights the way that vocation fights.**

---

### ⬟ THE KEEPER — *holds a thing so it does not fall*
**Ash Warden** (a death) · **Night-Warden** (a shelter) · **Green-Warden** (a wood) · **Hearth-Keeper** (a
place) · **Paladin** (a person) · **Peacemaker** (a peace) · **Hourkeeper** (a moment)

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you do not win, you REFUSE TO LOSE UNTIL THEY STOP.** `held_line` · `death_ward` ·
`shielding_light` · `stillness_field` · `in_the_way` · `deathless`
⛑ **CELL: PROTECT — Vantia of the Stillhold, nine wins with no offensive exchange.**

### ▲ THE MAKER — *builds*
**Artificer** (things) · **Lightwright** (light) · **Mason** (stone) · **Enginewright** (mechanism) ·
**Grafter** (flesh) · **Figurist** (form)

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you change the ground it happens on.** `raised_thing` · `boundary_stone` · `whats_at_hand` ·
`weapon_at_hand` · `blaze_wall` · `formcraft`
⛑ **CELL: SHAPE — Bricke of the Making, *"arrives with a satchel and permission to use it."***

### ◯ THE READER — *knows before acting*
**Wizard** · **Inquisitor** · **Lattice-Adept** · **Precursor-Delver** · **Storykeeper** · **Chaos-sense**

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you act ONCE, correctly, because you already know what they will do.** `read_the_fight` ·
`predicted_man` · `deduced_strike` · `foresee`-line · `convergent_strike`
⛑ **CELL: KNOW — Sethran, who calls your moves aloud a beat before you make them.**

### ◆ THE EDGE — *ends fights*
**Marcher** · **Samurai** · **Monk** · **Mourner-Killer** · **Blazeborn** · **Hungry**

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you are why the fight is short.** ⛔ **This is the one vocation whose answer is the obvious
one, and it is not privileged over the others.**
⛑ **CELL: HARM — Kestrin, and *"they are not here for blood."***

### ⬢ THE BROKER — *deals*
**Bargainer** · **Face** · **Advocate** · **Judge** · **Fae-Courtier**

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you end it on TERMS, and the terms are honest.** `struck_term` · `offered_price` · `lever` ·
`descent` · `broker_truce` · `case_closed`
⛑ **CELL: INFLUENCE — Lys, who *"does not appear to be fighting you"* and offers a real deal mid-bout.**
⚠️ **AND §C's ruling is what makes this live: bargain, persuade, provoke and soothe are combat verbs now.**

### ▸ THE WALKER — *goes where others do not*
**Wayfarer** · **Ranger** · **Thief** · **Lamplighter** · **Scout-Archer**

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you are not where the blow was going.** `loose_limbed` · `shadowstep` · `skydancer` ·
`the_way_out` · `kept_distance` · `never_there`
⛑ **CELL: MOVE — Aro, who has never been HIT in that arena. Not blocked. Hit.**

### ✚ THE ATTENDANT — *is present at what cannot be fixed*
**Mercy** · **Threnodist** · **Ash Warden** (its second vocation)

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you undo faster than they do.** `set_to_rights` · `administered_mercy` · `carried_weight` ·
`kept_breath` · `staunch`
⛑ **CELL: RESTORE — Oreth, who *"has never put a competitor down and has never lost by yielding."***

⚠️ **AND ERIK'S OWN EXAMPLE IS THE PROOF THAT THIS IS NOT A PACIFIST CLASS:** ⛔ ***"If you are an Attendant
you might also be a SPIRIT ASSASSIN."*** ⛑ **Attendant primary + Dark second: `the_attended_end` and
`deathsense` with `slow_cup`, `swallowed_word`, `stopped_breath`** — ⚠️ **a person whose whole vocation is
attending endings, who can also cause one. THE THREE-DOMAIN RULE MAKES IT LEGAL AND THE FICTION MAKES IT
FRIGHTENING.**

### ▬ THE ENDER — *ends THINGS, which is not the same as ending fights*
**Unmaker** · **Grave-Caller** · **Assassin**

⚑ **IN A FIGHT: you take away the thing they were fighting WITH.** `open_material` · `shatterpoint` ·
`ruinwork` · `unmaking_of_walls` · `clean_removal`
⛑ **CELL: SUSTAIN — Hallis, and the shape fits: *"neither may end this"* is a cell about what is still
standing when everything else is gone.**
⛔ **`demonstrated_end` is authored as *"the authority of someone who has shown they can END THINGS
CORRECTLY"* — that is a vocation, not a verb.**

## §3 — ⛔ THE NINTH VOCATION DOES NOT EXIST, AND IT IS THE ONE ERIK ASKED FOR

### ⛔ THE COMMANDER — *other people act on your word*

> **Erik: *"Perhaps the commander type has HIS PEOPLE FIGHT FOR HIM — even though he may be a capable
> fighter."***

⚠️ **EVERY VOCATION ABOVE IS SOMETHING YOU DO YOURSELF.** ⛔ **That is why 30 `command` crafts all command
ONE PERSON, ONE MACHINE OR ONE SPIRIT — the vocation was never there to hang a band-scale craft on.**

⛑ **AND THE HOLE SHOWS UP TWICE, INDEPENDENTLY:**
| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ **no craft** | `the_gathering` · `raise_banner` · `lead_the_line` · `command_field` — none exists |
| ⛔ **NO COLISEUM CELL** | ⚠️ **eight cells, eight families, and the ninth way of winning has no champion** |

⚑ **AND EVERYTHING ELSE IS ALREADY BUILT FOR IT:** the standing ladder · `delegationCapacity` · `bandOps` ·
the garrison · R46a's `legionClash` · **R36, which makes a party member fight from their own sheet** ·
`contributionsOf`, which already reads what someone contributes.

⛔ **THE COMMANDER IS THE ONE VOCATION THE ENGINE CAN ALREADY RESOLVE AND NO PLAYER CAN CHOOSE.**

## §4 — ⚑ WHY ALL EIGHT ARE VIABLE, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

⚠️ **Because a fight has five exits and only one of them is damage.** ⛑ **Health. Energy. Pressure to the
break. A death save. A yield.** ⛔ **The Keeper wins on pressure, the Broker on yield, the Walker by never
being reached, the Attendant by outlasting the damage, the Reader by making every exchange count and the
Maker by changing what the fight is happening ON.**

⚑ **AND THE COLISEUM IS THE ARGUMENT MADE PLAYABLE: eight cells, eight ways, and the encounter matrix
measures whether each one actually wins.** ⚠️ **It currently reports that threat explains the spread at
−0.95, which is a BALANCE problem and not a design one — ⛑ the ways are there; the numbers are not tuned.**
