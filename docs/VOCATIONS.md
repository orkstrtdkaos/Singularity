# The Vocations

*What a person is for, and how each one survives.*

Read this first, and *Archetypes, domain by domain* second. The vocations are the classes. The archetypes are
how each one looks in a given pole — a Keeper of the Ash is not a Keeper of the Hearth, but they are both
Keepers, and they survive the same way.

---

## A vocation is not a list of what your crafts do

Every craft in the game belongs to one of eight families by what it does in a round — protect, harm, move,
know, shape, restore, influence, sustain. Those are mechanics, and they are not vocations.

A Paladin and a Peacemaker both spend most of a fight protecting. They are not remotely the same job. One
stands in front of a person; the other takes the fight apart before it finishes. What separates them is not
what their crafts do but what they are for, and that is the thing a vocation names.

---

## The eight

In almost every game of this kind you have to be able to fight, and fighting is fun. Here, every one of these
eight is a way to win one. The Coliseum is the proof: eight cells, one for each family, each with a champion
who wins the way that vocation wins. Their names are below. You can go and watch them.

### ⬟ The Keeper — *holds a thing so it does not fall*

Ash Warden (a death) · Night-Warden (a shelter) · Green-Warden (a wood) · Hearth-Keeper (a place) ·
Paladin (a person) · Peacemaker (a peace) · Hourkeeper (a moment)

**In a fight,** you do not win. You refuse to lose until they stop.
Held Line · Death-Ward · Shielding Light · Stillness Field · In the Way · Deathless

*In the Coliseum:* Vantia of the Stillhold, in the Protect cell — nine wins, and not one offensive exchange.

### ▲ The Maker — *builds*

Artificer (things) · Lightwright (light) · Mason (stone) · Enginewright (mechanism) · Grafter (flesh) ·
Figurist (form)

**In a fight,** you change the ground it is happening on.
Raised Thing · Boundary-Stone · What's At Hand · Weapon at Hand · Blaze Wall · Formcraft

*In the Coliseum:* Bricke of the Making, in the Shape cell — who arrives with a satchel and permission to
use it.

### ◯ The Reader — *knows before acting*

Wizard · Inquisitor · Lattice-Adept · Precursor-Delver · Storykeeper · Chaos-sense

**In a fight,** you act once, correctly, because you already know what they will do.
Read the Fight · Predicted Man · Deduced Strike · Convergent Strike · the Foresee crafts

*In the Coliseum:* Sethran, in the Know cell — who calls your moves aloud a beat before you make them.

### ◆ The Edge — *ends fights*

Marcher · Samurai · Monk · Mourner-Killer · Blazeborn · Hungry

**In a fight,** you are why the fight is short.

This is the one vocation whose answer is the obvious one. It is not privileged over the others, and the seven
around it are not consolation prizes.

*In the Coliseum:* Kestrin, in the Harm cell — and they are not here for blood.

### ⬢ The Broker — *deals*

Bargainer · Face · Advocate · Judge · Fae-Courtier

**In a fight,** you end it on terms, and the terms are honest.
Struck Term · Offered Price · Lever · Descent · Broker Truce · Case Closed

Bargaining, persuading, provoking and soothing are combat actions here — taken in the round, against someone
who is trying to hit you. That is what makes this a way to win rather than a way to avoid playing.

*In the Coliseum:* Lys, in the Influence cell — who does not appear to be fighting you, and who will offer
you a real deal in the middle of the bout.

### ▸ The Walker — *goes where others do not*

Wayfarer · Ranger · Thief · Lamplighter · Scout-Archer

**In a fight,** you are not where the blow was going.
Loose-Limbed · Shadowstep · Skydancer · Way Out · Kept Distance · Never-There

*In the Coliseum:* Aro, in the Move cell — who has never been hit in that arena. Not blocked. Hit.

### ✚ The Attendant — *is present at what cannot be fixed*

Mercy · Threnodist · Ash Warden (its second vocation)

**In a fight,** you undo faster than they do.
Set to Rights · Administered Mercy · Carried Weight · Kept Breath · Staunch

This is not a pacifist class, and the clearest way to say so is to describe one. An Attendant whose second
domain is Dark carries Attended End and Deathsense alongside Slow Cup, Swallowed Word and Stopped Breath — a
person whose whole vocation is attending endings, who can also cause one. The three-domain rule makes it
legal. The rest is up to you.

*In the Coliseum:* Oreth, in the Restore cell — who has never put a competitor down and has never lost by
yielding.

### ▬ The Ender — *ends things, which is not the same as ending fights*

Unmaker · Grave-Caller · Assassin

**In a fight,** you take away the thing they were fighting with.
Open Material · Shatterpoint · Ruinwork · Unmaking of Walls · Clean Removal

There is a craft called Demonstrated End, and what it grants is the authority of someone who has shown they
can end things correctly. That is a description of a vocation rather than of a verb, and it is the clearest
thing written about what an Ender is.

*In the Coliseum:* Hallis, in the Sustain cell — a cell about what is still standing when everything else is
gone.

## Why all eight work

Because a fight has five exits, and only one of them is damage.

Health. Energy. Pressure to the break. A death save. A yield.

The Keeper wins on pressure. The Broker wins on the yield. The Walker wins by never being reached, the
Attendant by outlasting the damage, the Reader by making every exchange count, and the Maker by changing what
the fight is happening on. The Edge wins on health, which is the exit everyone already knew about.

Eight cells, eight ways through. Go and watch them.
