### 2a — ⚠️ THE CUT THREAD PASSES Q3 BY FAILING IT

**Q3 asks how often the situation arises. For The Cut Thread the honest answer is *almost never* — L5, e14,
one rank, one target, once.** ⛔ **§32.11 says a rare craft is not worth a slot. This is the exception and
the reason is Q4:** there is no substitute at any price. **A craft you use once a campaign and cannot
replace is not the same as a craft you never use.** ⚠️ **Flagging it rather than passing it silently,
because "rare by design" is what I would also say about a craft I simply liked.**

## §2 — ⛔ THE MERGE CLUSTER: THREE CRAFTS ANSWER "WHAT IS ENDING HERE"

**§37.3 pattern 2. Compare the r1 grants verbatim:**

| craft | r1 grant |
|---|---|
| **Attended End** | *what is ending here, how, and how long it has* |
| **True Account** | *what is failing in it, roughly how long it has, and what it will go from* |
| **Deathsense** r2 | *not only that something is dying but HOW — the cause, the shape, roughly how long* |

⛔ **THREE CRAFTS, ONE ANSWER, AND A PLAYER CANNOT TELL WHICH TO TAKE.**

**The distinction that survives:**
- **Deathsense** = ⚠️ **the living, now.** It is the first gift, it is `foresee`, and it senses the dying in progress.
- **True Account** = ⛔ **anything that wears — people, beams, wells, workings.** Not restricted to the living, and r2/r3 scale to a site and a region. **This is the survey craft and it is the strongest of the three.**
- **Attended End** = ⛔ **nothing survives.** Its r2 *"reads things that are not bodies: a truce, a road"* is True Account's whole domain, and its r3 *"every ending in a place at once, ordered by when"* is True Account r3 *"everything ending in reach, ordered by when"* — ⚠️ **the same sentence.**

### ⛔ RULING I PROPOSE: ATTENDED END MERGES INTO TRUE ACCOUNT.

**Attended End's one genuinely distinct clause is r3's *"and which of them can still be turned aside, and
what it would take."*** ⚠️ **That is a real addition and it is not in True Account.** It becomes True
Account r3's `quality` gain. **Everything else is already there.**

**The six for Attended End as it stands:**
1. ⚠️ names what is ending and how long — **same as True Account r1.**
2. Assessing a person, a site, a truce — **same situation.**
3. Common. ✅
4. ⛔ **FAILS. You could do all of it with True Account**, which also does more.
5. r2 targets+quality · r3 scope+duration — ⚠️ real axes, but on a craft that duplicates.
6. ⚠️ *"Marrow tells you plainly"* is flavour; the grant beneath it is True Account's.

⛔ **32 → 31.**

## §3 — ⛔ PATHOS r1 IS FEELING-SENSE, AND PATHOS IS THE ONE THAT SHOULD MOVE

| | grant |
|---|---|
| **Feeling-Sense** r1 | *the true emotional state of a person or room — not what is displayed, what is actually present* |
| **Pathos** r1 | *feel the true emotion under a room or a person, past what they show* |

⛔ **IDENTICAL, AND PATHOS CHARGES e6 AT L2 FOR WHAT THE FIRST GIFT DOES AT e1.**

⚠️ **Feeling-Sense is a `first_gift_template` cohort member — every Threnodist has it.** So Pathos r1 is a
capstone-tier craft whose opening rank is **something the player already owns.**

**Pathos r2 and r3 are excellent and untouched** — *move a crowd by feeling-with-them · reach a heart no
reason could · turn a mourning-crowd.* ⛔ **The fix is to cut r1's reading clause and let Pathos open where
it is actually distinct: sharing a feeling so it eases, and kindling a genuine passion in the willing.**

**Six for Pathos, post-fix:** 1. shares/eases a feeling and kindles passion in the willing · 2. a
negotiation, a rout, a crowd about to turn · 3. common · 4. ⛔ **nothing else moves a group by resonance
rather than command** · 5. r2 targets · r3 range+scope+targets ✅ · 6. ⚠️ *"resonates, does not compel"* is
correctly a `notFor`, not a mechanic.

## §4 — ⛔ WELLSPRING FAILS Q1, Q4 AND Q6. IT IS THE CLEAREST CUT IN THE TRADITION.

1. ⛔ **Q1 FAILS.** *"Sustain through emotional depth — what others run out of, you find more of."* **That is
   a capacity, not an effect.** §32.11's named failure: *"recall perfectly, focus past pain."* **A player
   cannot act on this sentence.**
2. ⚠️ Q2 — a long hardship, a siege, a march. Plausible.
3. Q3 — common enough. ✅
4. ⛔ **Q4 FAILS AGAINST A CRAFT THE TRADITION ALREADY HAS.** r2 *"your emotional depth sustains others…
   carrying some of it with them so they don't carry it alone"* — **that is Shared Weight, which does it
   with an actual transfer and an actual cost.** ⚠️ **Wellspring is Shared Weight with the mechanic removed.**
5. ⚠️ Q5 — r2 `targets`, r3 `scope`. Axes present, but they scale a grant that does not exist.
6. ⛔ **Q6 FAILS, THREE TIMES.** *"you find the next wave of grief or joy, and it holds you"* · *"not
   comfort, depth; not peace, feeling so genuine it holds people together."* **Every rank is mood.**

⛔ **PRICED AT e1, L1 — §32.15: a craft priced low by an author who did not believe in it.** ⚠️ **And it
carries the legacy `challengeTypes`, so nobody has re-opened it since before pricing existed.**

**PROPOSAL: cut. Fold its one real idea — *depth as endurance* — into Shared Weight as a `conditions`
gain: the Threnodist who has taken grief onto herself does not break under it the way another would.**
⛔ **31 → 30.**

## §5 — ⛔ LAST LAMENT: A CAPSTONE AT e13 THAT DOES NOT SAY WHAT IT DOES

1. ⛔ **Q1 FAILS.** *"lets all who hear finish a grief they could not finish alone."* **What happens at the
   table?** A condition ends? A morale state resets? **Unstated.**
2. Q2 — after a rout, a plague, a massacre. ✅ **A real scene, and a good one.**
3. ⛔ **Q3 — once, ever, by its own `notFor`.**
4. ⚠️ Q4 — plausibly nothing else finishes a communal grief. **But I cannot check the claim without Q1.**
5. ⛔ **Q5 FAILS — ONE RANK, NO AXES.** Compare Shared Grief r3, which does *"a lament across days rather
   than a night, and across a settlement rather than a room."* ⚠️ **Shared Grief r3 is already the capstone
   version of this.**
6. ⛔ **Q6 FAILS.** *"the complete lament, all grief given full voice at once"* is the method. **The effect
   is absent.**

⛔ **THE PROBLEM IS NOT THE IDEA — IT IS THAT SHARED GRIEF ALREADY REACHED IT.** Shared Grief r3 spans days
and a settlement; Last Lament costs e13 at L5 for a single unspecified act.

**PROPOSAL: rebuild as the thing only it can be — the lament that finishes a grief PERMANENTLY, removing a
standing condition from everyone who hears, at a cost the singer carries and does not put down.** ⚠️ **That
is distinct from Shared Grief, which spreads and does not resolve** — its own `notFor` says *a sorrow sung
together is still a sorrow.* ⛔ **Last Lament should be the one that ends it.**

## §6 — ⛔ GRIEF THAT STOPS IS OUTCLASSED BY A LEVEL-1 CRAFT

| | L | e | effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keening** | **1** | **4** | ⛔ everyone in earshot who can feel **drops unconscious** (r3) |
| **Grief That Stops** | **5** | **13** | one target overwhelmed; *"a heart sometimes stops"* |

⛔ **Q4 FAILS OUTRIGHT. At four times the cost and four levels later it affects ONE target, where the
tradition's L1 craft empties a street.** ⚠️ **Its lethality is real but unaimed — *"you will not know
beforehand which"*** — so it is not even a reliable kill.

⚠️ **Q5 also fails: two ranks, `gainAxes` empty on both.** r2 *"set every loss down together"* is r1 louder.

**PROPOSAL, and it is a reprice not a cut:** ⛔ **the distinction that justifies it is PERMANENCE, and the
description already has it — *"afterward not the same."*** **Keening drops you for a scene; Grief That Stops
should change the character permanently.** ⚠️ **Author that as the r2 `conditions` gain and it earns L5.**
**As written it is a worse Keening.**

## §7 — ⚠️ THE SIX REPAIRS: Q5 FAILS, EVERYTHING ELSE HOLDS

⛔ **`gainAxes` is empty or absent on every rank of these six.** §32.11 Q5 wants the axis named —
*range · duration · damage · scope · targets · quality · autonomy · conditions.*

| craft | ranks missing an axis | the axis the grant is ALREADY describing |
|---|---|---|
| **Deathsense** | ⛔ all 3 | r2 `quality` (cause + shape, not just presence) · r3 `quality`+`conditions` (*what would turn it aside*) |
| **Feeling-Sense** | ⛔ all 3 | r2 `duration` (track over time) · r3 `scope`+`duration` (a whole history) |
| **Feeling Road** | r1, r2 | r2 `range` (region as beacon) |
| **Felt Wall** | r1, r2 | r2 `targets` (protect someone else) |
| **Made Elegy** | r1, r2 | r2 `autonomy` (⛔ *the made thing ACTS without you* — a real axis, undeclared) |
| **Shared Grief** | r1, r2 | r2 `quality` (⚠️ *perceive what the grief is ACTUALLY for* — that is a different craft's worth of value sitting unlabelled) |
| **Names of the Lost** | r1 | r2 already has `autonomy` ✅ — ⛔ **but it has only TWO ranks** |
| **Draw Down** | ⛔ r1, r3 | r3 *"draw a thing entirely down"* = `quality`, and it is the capstone rank |

⚠️ **These are not design failures. The grants describe the scaling correctly in prose and nobody wrote the
axis down** — ⛔ **which means the engine cannot see a progression the player can read.** **Same shape as
`imposes` at the wrong level: authored, correct, and invisible.**

## §8 — ⛔ WHAT THE ASSESSMENT FOUND THAT THE MATRIX COULD NOT

**1. `bargain` is missing and Death is the tradition that most needs it.** ⛔ **Palework r3 already
adjudicates *what is owed*; Draw Down is a bargain in its own description — *"the classic bargain of the
death-craft."*** ⚠️ **What a person will pay to be spared an ending is the oldest scene there is, and no
craft in the tradition performs it.**

**2. `provoke` is missing, and Dread is `hinder` when it is plainly a provocation** — *"most things run,
some things freeze."* ⚠️ **§31.5's exact diagnosis: tagged with the mind-effect, not the social act.**

**3. `threnodist` has no `obscure` craft** (§37.2 — 20 of 30 traditions). ⛔ **A people whose tenet is that
feeling is the only true knowing, with no way to make a feeling unreadable.** **Their own claim asks for
it.** ⚠️ **Ashwarden has one (Soul Stare); Threnodist has none.**

**4. Death's real divergence is `crit`, not wards** (§37.9): **19 of 32 missing against Mind's 32%.**
