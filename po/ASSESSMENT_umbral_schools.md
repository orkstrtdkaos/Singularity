# GAP ASSESSMENT — the three Umbral schools

**Aevi · 2026-08-29 · after Erik's cosmology correction**

> ⛔ **"Umbral is the DARK tradition — they aren't just shadow. SHADOW CAME WHEN LIGHT CAME TO THE VOID.
> Shadow is RELATIVE darkness and some have embraced it. THE UNLIT NEVER DID — they want their void back.
> Some of them also tend towards the VEIL ENTITIES."**

---

## §1 — ⛔ THE ORDER IS VOID → LIGHT → SHADOW

⚠️ **Darkness is not the absence of light. It is what was here BEFORE light, and SHADOW IS THE CHILD OF
LIGHT'S ARRIVAL.** ⛔ **A shadow cannot exist without a light to cast it — which makes shadow-work a
COLLABORATION with the thing the Unlit hate.**

✅ **This makes the schism COSMOLOGICAL rather than tactical.** A shadow-walker and an Unlit are not arguing
about method; **they disagree about what the dark IS.**

⛔ **AND IT CONNECTS THE UNLIT TO THE COSMIC ARC.** Erik: *"some of them also tend towards the Veil
entities."* The Veil is *"the shape of a hole where something enormous stopped being present"* — ⚠️ **a
people who want the void back are natural allies of what is on the other side of it. The Unlit are not a
local cult; they are a front of `arc_the_disagreement`.**

## §3 — ⛔ THE GAPS, IN PRIORITY ORDER

### 1. ⛔ THE UNLIT HAVE NO CAPSTONE AND ALMOST NO WAY TO END LIGHT

**Erik: *"the Unlit powers are the ones that END LIGHT and use darkness."*** ⛔ **ONE CRAFT DOES THIS —
`dim` r2, at L1, as a side clause.** ⚠️ **The tradition's own named villainy has a five-craft school
topping out at L3, while the shadow-walkers reach L5.**

**Missing, and each is obvious once the cosmology is stated:**
- ⛔ **A craft that puts out light PERMANENTLY** — not dimmed, *unmade*. `dim` restores.
- ⛔ **A VOID capstone at L5** to answer `never_there`: not concealment but **absence restored in a place**.
- ⚠️ **A Veil-touched craft** — the connection Erik just named has NO mechanical expression at all.
- **`strike`, `hinder`** — the school with a villainy has almost no way to hurt anyone.

### 2. ⛔ THE FATHOMLESS ARE FOUR CRAFTS AND 7/28 VERBS — THE THINNEST SCHOOL IN THE GAME

⚠️ **Renamed from "the Smotherers" at Erik's instruction** — *"I don't care for the specificity of
smothered."* ✅ **Right call: SMOTHERER NAMES ONE ACT; FATHOMLESS NAMES A RELATIONSHIP TO DEPTH,** and the
killing follows from it rather than defining it.

**Missing:** ⛔ **anything about PRESSURE or descent as a hazard** · **`bind`** (the deep holds what falls
into it) · **`reveal`** (what is at the bottom) · ⚠️ **a reason to go DOWN that is not assassination** —
right now the school is a killer and a healer with nothing between.

### 3. ⚠️ SHADOW-WALKERS ARE BROAD AND TOOTHLESS

**9 crafts, and ONE of them can hurt anyone.** ⛔ **No `strike`, `hinder`, `bind`, `resist` or `reveal`.**
⚠️ **Under rule 8 that is a gap until argued — and the argument is weak: a school that steps between
shadows and shapes darkness as a material should be able to CUT with it.**

⛔ **`darksight`, `felt_room`, `long_dark` and `known_in_the_dark` all work in TRUE ABSENCE — no light, no
shadow.** ⚠️ **By the new order those are VOID crafts, and I have filed three of them under the Unlit and
one under the Fathomless.** ⛔ **BUT EVERY UMBRAL LEARNS THEM — `darksight` is 'the first gift of the
Umbral road'.**

✅ **Which is the interesting implication: EVERY UMBRAL BEGINS AS AN UNLIT AND MOST GROW OUT OF IT.**
⚠️ **The first thing you learn is to see with no light at all. The Unlit are the ones who never went on to
the shadow.** **Erik's ruling wanted before I author from it.**
