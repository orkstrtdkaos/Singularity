## PART 1 — THE BRAID RULE IS GONE, AND ERIK IS RIGHT
I wrote: *"a braid that escaped its parents' limits would be a way to launder bounds."* Erik: **"Braiding to
launder bounds is exactly why you would want combinations."**
**He's right and my rule was self-defeating.** A combination that can only do what both halves could **is not a
combination — it is a discount.** My inheritance rule quietly made 21 recipes pointless the moment I wrote it.
**FIXED (live):** every `notFor` now states what **THE BRAID** cannot do. Seven explicitly **go past a parent**,
and those are the ones worth having:
- **`the_unfinished`** holds a body **past its ending** — which neither `palework` (*"never raises"*) nor
  `the_kept_breath` (*"holds the threshold, does not move it"*) could alone. **That is the entire point of it.**
- `the_kept_absence` **defends**, which concealment alone never does · `the_clean_end` binds **cleanly** where
  the soldier's hand alone always binds wrong · `the_read_strike` lands **before force arrives** ·
  `the_lucky_break` lets you choose **roughly** where wildcraft cannot choose at all · `the_answered_judgment`
  **holds** where ascent binds-and-leaves · `the_named_price` is fair **and witnessed**.
Each still has a real limit — **what the braid can't reach.** Bounded, not inherited.

## PART 2 — THE BIAS AUDIT. THE COUNT IS SMALL; THE PLACEMENT IS BAD; THE FEEDBACK LOOP IS THE REAL PROBLEM.
### MEASURED
**22 of 806 live bounds are ethical/taste-shaped rather than mechanical** (~2.7%). **15 of those 22 are HARD** —
the class that says *no mastery ever reaches this.*
In my staged work: **16 of 819**, and **13 of 16 are HARD.**

**They sit on identity crafts.** A taste-bound on a minor craft is a footnote; a HARD taste-bound on a
tradition's **core** craft *defines the tradition*:

| **`the_broken_rhythm`** | ***"it STOPS, it does not HARM"*** | ⚠️ **I authored this THIS SESSION**, minutes after Erik told me somatic should have *"crazy damage skills."* I fixed the damage gap and then immediately wrote a no-harm bound onto the control craft. |

### ⚠️ THE ACTUAL ERROR, AND IT IS WORSE THAN THE BOUNDS
**I read a pattern, amplified it by authoring more of it, then cited the amplified count as evidence the
pattern was real.**
I reported *"seven traditions refuse coercion"* → then *"eleven"* → then *"thirteen traditions refuse coercion
mechanically, and none of them reference each other — the most robust cross-tradition value in the catalog."*
**Several of those were mine.** I wrote `the_deep_covenant`'s bound and then counted abyssal as independent
corroboration. **That is a feedback loop dressed as a finding**, and it is the more serious failure — the
bounds can be edited; a false pattern shapes everything authored after it.

## PART 3 — THE RULE GOING FORWARD
**A bound states what a craft CANNOT REACH. It does not state what a practitioner SHOULD NOT WANT.**
- ✅ *"it reaches only where life runs unbroken"* — a limit of the craft.
- ✅ *"the ordered and prepared resist it"* — a limit of the craft.
- ❌ *"you ask — always; nothing here is compelled"* — **a limit of manners.**
- ❌ *"never raises"* — **a limit of taste**, and one the tradition's own name argues against.
**AND THE WORLD ARGUES AGAINST ME HERE, WHICH IS THE TELL.** It already contains the Hollow Market, a licence
trade with forgers inside the Orders, god-named who took names as armour, `draw_down` (*"their wound is your
mending"*), `the_dread` (*"some of them will never be right again"*), and `the_cut_thread`. **The catalog is
gritty, blunt and morally serious. I was writing a politer world than the one I was standing in.**

## PART 4 — THE REMEDIATION PASS (proposed, not yet executed)
**~22 live + 16 staged bounds to revisit.** Three verdicts per bound:
1. **KEEP** — it's genuinely mechanical, my regex just matched the phrasing (e.g. `false_trail`'s *"it must end
   somewhere"* is about the trail, not ethics).
2. **DEMOTE hard → soft/cost** — the limit is real but a master should reach past it. *"Cannot change anyone's
   mind"* becomes **hard to do, and it costs**, not impossible.
3. **DELETE and re-author** — the bound is manners. **`palework`'s "never raises" goes**, and the ashwarden
   gets its raising back — which the new `the_unfinished` and `the_kept_hand` already assume.
**AND ADD WHAT THE BIAS SUPPRESSED:** Erik's framing — *"the death people should have skills that work with
death, so should life"* — means **ashwarden is short a raising line, rootkin short a life-imposing line, and
the social traditions short anything that actually takes a decision away from someone.** The gap isn't only
over-bounded crafts; **it's crafts I never wrote because I'd already decided the tradition wouldn't.**
**ERIK'S CALL on scope: bounds-only pass, or bounds + the missing crafts?** The second is larger and I think
it's the real answer.
