### ⛔ AND IT HAS ALREADY PRODUCED A LIVE COLLISION

id already disagrees with its display name, so it is the cheaper side.**

## §2 — THE NINEFOLD ADJUDICATION

**Three figures carry "Ninefold". Only one earns it.**

| figure | what it is | does "Ninefold" fit? |
|---|---|---|
| **epic `the_cogitant_ninefold`** | ⛔ **NINE minds that were one engineer**, distributed. tradition **enginewright**, home `the_great_engine`, part-machine, they/them | ✅ **YES — nine is the entire conceit** |
| **epic `the_ninefold_ascendant`** | a **cogitant** who dissolved into pure thought and would take everyone out of the flesh. Legendary villain | ⛔ **NO. Nothing in the record is nine.** Its idea is dissolution, not multiplicity |
| **authored `cogitant_ninefold`** | **ONE man** — thin to translucence, forties, bald by choice, lattice under the skin | ⛔ **NO. He is one person.** |

### ✅ THE RULING

**"Ninefold" belongs to the nine enginewright minds and to nothing else.** ⛔ **But that figure is also
mis-labelled: it is called "The COGITANT Ninefold" while its own record says `tradition: enginewright` and
its signature says "nine ENGINEWRIGHT minds arguing as one."**

➡️ **So all three names are wrong, in three different ways:**

1. ⬜ **The nine minds keep "Ninefold" and lose "Cogitant"** — it names the wrong tradition.
2. ⬜ **`the_ninefold_ascendant` loses "Ninefold"** — its idea is a mind shedding a body, and *ascendant*
   already carries that. **The nine was borrowed.**
3. ⬜ **The authored man loses "Ninefold"** — he is one person, and his own text gives better material:
   *"elegant answers that leave out everything the body knows."*

⛔ **NOT EXECUTED. All three are save-affecting** — `the_cogitant_ninefold` appears as an active pursuit in
**five live saves** and in a sixth chronicle line, and the authored NPC now carries a sheet, `sex` and
`assistTags`. ➡️ **This is a rename migration, which is CCode's mechanism, not a content edit.**
⬜ **And the new names are Erik's to approve — I have demonstrably poor judgement about which of my names
are the same name.**

## §3 — ⬜ THE GUARD I OWE

⚠️ **The corpus is now large enough that I cannot hold it in view, and this is the second time in two days a
name collision was found by someone else** (CCode found the Ninefolds; Erik named the pattern).

➡️ **Proposed check, small and mine to specify:**
1. ⛔ **A new craft/figure/creature name that MATCHES an existing display name fails.** `kept_vigil` /
   `long_watch` would have been caught at authoring.
2. ⚠️ **A warning — not a failure — when an opener passes a threshold.** *"'last' now opens 12 names."*
   **A warning rather than a gate, because the construction is not wrong; using it eleven times without
   noticing is.**

⬜ **Cheap, and it turns a thing only Erik can currently see into something the build says out loud.**
