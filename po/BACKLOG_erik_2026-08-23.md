## ORDERED BY WHAT IS BLOCKED, NOT BY SIZE

### ⛔ 1 · THE SKILL WHEEL ON THE CREATE SCREEN — *the smallest, and it is nearly free*

**The wheel Erik asked for already exists.** CCODE-196/197 shipped filter-to-hide, multi-select traditions
with second-click-off, verb chips, and filter-by-every-function. **It lives in `renderPlay`'s surface.**

⚠️ **The create screen does not use it.** `renderCreate` (app.js 4429–5129) references the wheel **once**;
the wheel's own model builder is at 8984. **Two surfaces, one of them a generation behind.**

**Owner: CCode.** ⚠️ **Not a rebuild — a re-point**, and the risk is that `buildWheelModel` assumes a
character with owned crafts and creation has none yet. **Measure that first.**

### ⛔ 4 · THE MAPS — *the largest content gap on the list, and it is measurable*

| | |
|---|---|
| regions in play | **38** |
| with an authored region map | ⛔ **8** |
| locations on the world map | **135** |
| with a local layout | ⛔ **18** |

**The world map is done** — `terrain.json` carries all 135 locations, 27 biomes, hydrology, 38 seats.
**What is missing is the two zoom levels below it.**

⚠️ **Thirty regions have no map**, including `the_ascent`, `the_pattern_reach`, `radiant_wastes` and six
foothills. **117 locations have no local layout.**

**Owner: split.** The generator (`scripts/world/`) is CCode's; **the 30 region maps and 117 layouts are
authoring**, and at 4–6 sites each that is the biggest single body of work on this list.
⛔ **Worth asking whether the GM should GENERATE a layout on first visit rather than authoring 117** — which
makes this item 7 in disguise.

### ⛔ 5 · NPCs WHO ACT — *the widest gap between authored and alive*

| | |
|---|---|
| authored NPCs | **111** |
| with `interiority` (a want, a fear, an inner life) | ⛔ **7** |
| with a quest | ⛔ **2** |

**A hundred and four people exist and do nothing.** ⚠️ **`drivenNpcDirective` is 890 characters of

**Owner: split, and the engine half is the smaller one.** ⛔ **What does not exist is a REASON for the
engine to bring an NPC to the player** — a wants-something-from-you clock, the way `projectTicks` is a
work clock. **Aevi's 890 characters are waiting on a trigger.**

### ⛔ 6 · DUNGEONS ACROSS THE WORLD — *blocked on 7, and Aevi has started it*

**There is no dungeon record type.** The Sunk Assay is the first one and it is authored as a spec, four
levels, three-quarters on origin. **SNG-529 is her templating the dungeon kind for GM spawning.**

⚠️ **"Populate the world" is not "author fifty dungeons".** ⛔ **It is item 7 aimed at one content type** —
a dungeon TEMPLATE the GM can spawn against a place, a threat and a claim, the way it already spawns
encounters. **The Sunk Assay is the worked example that proves the shape.**

**Owner: Aevi's template, CCode's spawner. Do 7 first.**

---

### ⛔ 7 · EVERY CONTENT TYPE GENERATABLE — *the one that makes 4 and 6 tractable*

**`po/CATALOGUE_gm_content.md` is already the audit: 14 things the model generates at runtime against what
it is given to generate FROM.** 13 of 14 had a template; the companion gap is closed.

⛔ **So "ensure" is now a GATE, not an audit** — a check that every catalogued type has a live template,
a live reader, and a spawn path, so a fifteenth type cannot arrive with two of the three.

⚠️ **AND IT IS THE KEYSTONE FOR TWO OTHER ITEMS.** If a local layout can be generated on first visit, item
4 stops being 117 authoring jobs. If a dungeon can be spawned against a place, item 6 stops being fifty.
**Neither is true today, and both become true through this one.**

**Owner: CCode (the gate + the spawn paths), Aevi (any template the gate finds missing).**
