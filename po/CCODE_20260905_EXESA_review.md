## §3 — ⚠️ THREE THINGS THAT ARE TRUE BUT LOSE SOMETHING

### 3a · ⛔ *"NANITE… is the one source with no favourite ground at all"* — **TRUE OF ORDERED, NOT OF WILD**

**`ordered_nanite`** ✅ *"it answers to CONDITION AND SUPPLY, not to ground: **no band, no floor**"* — the
doc's sentence is the authored sentence.

⛔ **But `wild_nanite` has band {0.32, 0.34} and *"thrives in the unreached gaps."*** ⚠️ **That IS a
favourite ground**, and the doc has just told the reader the source has none. ➡️ ⬜ **One clause fixes it:
*"ordered nanite has no favourite ground; wild nanite has one — the gaps."***

### 3b · ⚑ AND THE SAME PARAGRAPH DROPS THE BEST STORY IN IT

> *"NANITE is the made copy: ordered where someone maintains it, wild where nobody does."*

⛔ **The content says something much better:** *"ORDERED AND WILD ARE ONE SOURCE IN TWO STATES. Rootkin and
Churnfolk break ordered → wild; Seraphim and Enginewrights order wild → ordered. **⚠️ THAT CONVERSION IS A
LIVE FACTIONAL ARGUMENT OVER A SHARED RESOURCE**, not two peoples using different materials."*

⚑ **Wild is not neglect. It is somebody's WORK, and four traditions are fighting over which direction the
same material should run.** ⬜ **That is a whole faction conflict the doc currently reads as entropy**, and
it belongs in a document whose §V is about what is moving.

⛔ **It asks a great deal of the place — just a different thing.** Band **{0.15, 0.22}** is a real thin-ground
