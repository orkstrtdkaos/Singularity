## M4 — the field stops drowning the land ✅

Measured through the painter's own call, **including its ×0.9**: at your stated ambient of 0.27 the stipple
put **2,409 dots on a 977×513 frame**. Density *is* the value, which is right — and a field that is weakly
**everywhere** is the common case, so proportionality alone fills the frame.

`stipple` now takes a `floor` and a `cap`:

| | motes |
|---|---|
| your ambient 0.27, before | **2,409** |
| after (floor 0.18, cap 420) | **420** — −83% |
| weak ground 0.12 | **0** |

⛑ **The cap sorts and keeps the strongest**, never the first — the dots are generated in scan order, so
truncating the list would bias every capped frame to its top-left corner with nothing to say so.

⚠️ **The floor is tuned, not chosen, and the number matters to you.** At **0.30** the valley drew *nothing at
all* — more than you asked for, and it would have removed a layer Erik can see today. At **0.18** your ambient
survives and is capped while genuinely weak ground goes bare: the **cap** does the sparseness, the **floor**
does the bareness. All three are dials in `FIELD_LOOK` (`wildFloor`, `wildCap`, `washMult`) and the look is
yours — turn them. Wash opacity is halved as asked.

## M5 — the powers stop sharing a hue ✅, and it was not what the note assumed

⛔ **It is a collision, not a missing path.** The fill and the border have *always* used the power's own
colour. But **0 of 41 powers carry an authored one**, so all 41 hash into a 12-hue palette — and `#8f87c2`, a
violet, comes out for **seven of them**, Millbrook's Elder Panel among them.

A global hash promises a *stable* colour per id; it cannot promise that two powers **side by side** differ.
So the frame now assigns, largest ground first, and the powers you can see at once are always distinct.

⚠️ **An authored colour still wins outright** — so your "Fellowship brown, Castellany teal, Elder Panel blue"
is now a content edit that the engine will honour, and it is the only way to pin a *specific* power to a
*specific* hue. Nothing else needs to change for it.
