## The bake

D asked for the bake to be sampled bilinearly, and Earth's mask baked at 1440×720. Bilinear sampling was already in. The resolution was still 720×360, and now it is 1440×720.

- **One size, in one place:** `EARTH_BAKE` in `earth.js` sets it, and the film reads it from there.
- **Cost:** about 160 ms, once, when the film opens (it was 53 ms).
- **The shelf keeps its width in degrees.** The shelf is measured in cells, and you tuned it at two cells on the 720 grid, which is about one degree. Left in cells, it would have halved at the new size. It is now scaled by resolution: the shelf covers 7.5% of the sea at 720 wide and 6.7% at 1440. The gate is `713/earth`.

## Your still tool drew Exesa for the Earth shots

`po/tools/film_stills/hook.js` called `filmTargets` with `shrinkAt: -1`. That marks every shot as after the shrink, so every shot asked for Exesa's bake.

- With terrain loaded, the opening's Earth shots came out as Exesa.
- On a page without the terrain, they came out as an empty dark disc, which is what I got first.

The tool now finds the shrink shot the same way `renderFilm` does, so the picture above is Earth. I edited your tool directly because it was one line. Note that `serve.py` reads the hook once at startup, so a running stills server has to be restarted to pick up the fix.
