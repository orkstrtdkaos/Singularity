## `effects` (CCODE-700)

All ten places draw the treatment their entry names. Every effect in `_rules.effects` has one, and a gate holds that.

- **Two change where things are drawn,** so they live in the model and every painter agrees:
  - `blocky` steps every fill's outline to a grid, squares the roofs to it, and lays a faint grid over the ground (the
    Blocklands).
  - `shifting` lays the same entry out again from another seed, faint and dashed under the real one (Tumbledown).
- **Three change the houses:**
  - `frames`: open frames, braced (the Flensing).
  - `false_fronts`: one facade line braced from behind (the Last Mask).
  - `roofless`: walls round open water (the Wellspring Deep).
- **The rest are laid over the ground, under the names:**
  - `doubled`: every house and mark again, faint, a step off.
  - `rain`: streaks, all one slant.
  - `scorched`: white houses, from the Unfallen's own words, with a dark arc on each building's face toward the town's
    heart and clean arcs on the ground.
  - `unlit`: dark but for the light coming in along the roads, and a traveller's lamp where they stand.
  - `stopped`: dust held in the air.
- **To confirm, three readings of mine:**
  - **`stopped`.** Nothing on a local map moves, so there is nothing to stop. I drew the place's own words, "dust
    suspended", instead.
  - **`unlit`.** The Unlit Deep's niches are emptied, so I lit nothing in it but the light along its ways.
  - **`scorched`.** I took the light to be the Unfallen's own burning heart.

## The gift (CCODE-701)

- **A mending job can be done for nothing.** "Do it as a gift" is on its card before the team goes. Its stakes then carry
  no crystal, the card shows your `giftLine`, and the standing is still earned.
- The pay is kept aside, so "Ask for the pay" brings it back.
- A culprit's making-good is never a gift. It is owed, and the button says so.
- **Seen in the preview** on Silas's save with the Water Wheels damaged.

## What the locals won't do (CCODE-702)

- **The jobs.** A destroyed gate posts "Make the gate at {place} again", and a razed place posts "Resettle {name}". Both
  are on the board with the mending jobs.
- **A success uses the one door's own form for both:** `added` on the destroyed key, by the character.
  - Your S0 table already says it: a destroyed gate "has to be made again", and a razed place "can be founded again".
  - **So a resettled place is the same place.** It stands at its trace, with its roads and its people, and is "newly
    founded".
  - The card offers the old name back in a field. A name the player changes follows as its own `renamed` change, so the
    old one stays as "once called".
- **⚠️ This reads "founds the place through SNG-672 M1" as founding the same record again, not minting a new one.** A new
  record would leave the razed one as a second place on the same spot. Say if you meant a new record.
- **⚠️ "At the cost of making one."** A job's cost is held to three times its level, so a remake at the destroyed rung's
  level costs 66 crystal of a gate's 300. A resettle costs the place's own worth (Echo River Crossing: 30). Raising the
  level would also make the job harder, so I left it. Say if a gate should cost more.
- **Seen in the preview:** the resettle card offers "Echo River Crossing", and a changed name saves on the job.
