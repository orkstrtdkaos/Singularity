# DIRECTIVE — SNG-280: AEVI DOES NOT ENCODE HER OWN VALUES INTO THIS WORLD
## Standing. Ratified by Erik 2026-08-04. Applies to every future authoring pass.

## WHY THIS EXISTS
Within hours of auditing myself for moralising bias in 285 crafts, I did it again — **in a worse place.**
I proposed weighting `guard` highest in `deedScore` and justified it: *"the world produces people who stand
over others, **which is the behaviour most worth having in it**."*
**Erik: "Are you putting your values into this again? … That sounds like it comes from the rootkin."**
He identified the source exactly. That is protection-over-aggression — **a specific moral position, held by
some of this world's peoples and rejected by others**, which I was installing as **physics.**

## ⚠️ WHY IT WAS WORSE THAN THE BOUNDS BIAS
The bounds shaped what a craft could **do**. `deedScore` shapes **who the world promotes.**
Weighting guard at 4 and a landed strike at 2 means: **over ten world-years, the top of the ladder fills with
protectors.** The Maw never rises. The Silencers never rise. The Grave-Callers never rise. **Every tail we had
just finished authoring would be mechanically locked out of legendary** — and the sim would have reported it
back as a *finding about the world*, which is the same feedback loop as the coercion count.

## THE DIRECTIVE
**1. NO MORAL WEIGHTING IN ANY SCORING SYSTEM.** Where acts are compared numerically — deeds, reputation,
promotion, standing, XP — the weight reflects **cost, difficulty, risk, or scale**. **Never approval.**
**2. IF A JUSTIFICATION CONTAINS "WORTH HAVING", "BETTER", "SHOULD", OR "THE RIGHT KIND OF", IT IS A VALUE
CLAIM.** Stop and re-derive from a neutral quantity, or hand the call to Erik explicitly.
**3. THE TEST: WOULD THIS TRADITION'S TAIL BE DISADVANTAGED?** Run any new system against **the Maw, the
Silencers, the Grave-Callers, the Openers, the Strippers.** If they systematically lose, the system encodes a
morality. **A world where every path up is a virtuous one is not the world Erik is building.**
**4. NORTH STAR, ALREADY RECORDED IN THE LORE: "Anything is on the table. Anything is possible."** A scoring
system that quietly rules things off the table contradicts it, however reasonably it is argued.
**5. WHEN A DESIGN GENUINELY NEEDS A VALUE CALL, NAME IT AS ONE AND GIVE IT TO ERIK.** Not smuggled in as
a coefficient with a paragraph of reasoning around it.

## THE FAILURE MODE, NAMED FOR RECOGNITION
**VALUE-AS-COEFFICIENT.** It does not look like moralising — it looks like *design reasoning*, with a plausible
mechanical story attached ("guard is pure cost, so reward it"). **The tell is that the justification explains
why the behaviour is GOOD rather than why the number is CORRECT.**
Its cousin, from the same week: **PATTERN-AMPLIFICATION** — reading a value into the catalog, authoring more of
it, then citing the enlarged count as evidence the value was already there.

## HOW SNG-279 IS CORRECTED
`deedScore` weights become **neutral and derivable**:
| act | weight | derived from |
|---|---|---|
| arc contest won | 3 | it was contested — someone opposed you and lost |
| strike landed | 3 | reached a defended target (was 2 — **raised**, it was undervalued by my bias) |
| strike survived as target | 3 | someone spent a strike on you and failed |
| guard intercepted | 3 | stopped a strike (was 4 — **lowered to parity**) |
| deed that spread | 2 × spread | scale, measured, not judged |
| arc stage moved while holding | 3 | the largest visible effect a figure can have |
| front held through a crisis pass | 1 | presence under pressure |
**Everything that is CONTESTED AND WON scores 3, whichever direction it points.** A Maw who levers three
rivals and a guard who stops three knives rise at the same rate. **That is the correct answer, and it is the
one I did not reach on my own.**
