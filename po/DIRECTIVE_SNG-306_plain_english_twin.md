# DIRECTIVE — SNG-306: EVERY EVOCATIVE LINE NEEDS A PLAIN-ENGLISH TWIN
## Standing. Ratified by Erik 2026-08-04. Applies to all authored content, retroactively.

## THE COMPLAINT, AND IT IS CORRECT
> Erik: *"look at the words that describe these skills… WHAT DO THEY MEAN??? It's so abstract and vague.
> Flavor text is good, but if you look at all this stuff it's almost always so vague you can't tell what it
> means. 'It is their hand, and both of you know it'???? WHAT? If you have this type of content it HAS to have
> a plain english speak translation too."*
**He is right, and here is the proof, from a craft I wrote yesterday.** Everything a player can read about
`the_closing_argument`:
- **description:** *"walks a person, honestly, from premises they already hold to a conclusion that ends them"*
- **r2 grants:** *"Reach the conclusion where the consistent act is a final one — and STOP TALKING."*
- **surge:** *"reach the end; IT IS THEIR HAND, AND BOTH OF YOU KNOW IT"*
- **bounds:** *"THE UNREASONABLE ARE IMMUNE"* · *"YOU DO NOT PERFORM IT."*
**⚠️ NOWHERE IN ANY OF THAT does it say WHAT ROLL, AGAINST WHAT, WHAT HAPPENS ON SUCCESS, or WHAT THE TARGET
CAN DO ABOUT IT.** A player cannot decide whether to use this craft. **That is not atmosphere; it is a missing
rule wearing atmosphere.**

## THE FAILURE MODE, NAMED
**EVOCATION-WITHOUT-OPERATION.** A line that *sounds* like it means something, carries real feeling, and
**contains no information the player can act on.** It is seductive to write and it passes every check I have —
CI validates that a bound EXISTS, never that it SAYS anything.
**The tell:** if you cannot answer *"so what do I do differently?"* from the line alone, it is evocation.
**And my own worst examples, so this is not abstract:**
| line | what it fails to say |
|---|---|
| *"it is their hand, and both of you know it"* | who rolls, against what, and what the target may do |
| *"leans on it"* | how much, on what, with what effect |
| *"the two poles fight"* | fight how — a cost? a penalty? a chance of failure? |
| *"what you set outlives your attention"* | for how long, and can it be stopped |
| *"nothing about it looks like violence"* | is this a stealth bonus or only colour? |

## THE RULE
**EVERY authored line that carries a mechanic must have a PLAIN twin, in the same record.**
- **`plainly`** — one or two sentences, **no metaphor, no capitals-for-drama, no second clause that reframes
  the first.** It says: **what you roll, against what, what happens if you succeed, and what the target can
  do.**
- The evocative line **stays**. It is not the enemy — Erik: *"flavor text is good."* **The failure is flavour
  text with nothing underneath it.**
- **This applies to: craft `description`, each rank's `grants`, each `intensity` line, and every `bounds`
  entry.** A bound especially: *a limit nobody can apply is not a limit.*

## WORKED — the same craft, repaired
```
description: "⚠️ THE CORNERS' CRAFT. A Syllogist walks a person, honestly, from premises they
              already hold to a conclusion that ends them…"
plainly:     "A SOCIAL contest against the target's resolve. You must be able to state a sound
              argument from something they already believe. On a win they cannot act against that
              conclusion for the scene. At rank 2, if the conclusion is that a final act is the only
              consistent one, the GM resolves whether the target performs it — you never roll for
              that. The target can refuse to engage at any point, and anyone who will not reason is
              immune outright."
```
**Same craft. Same feeling. Now usable.**

## WHY I KEEP DOING IT — worth recording so it can be watched for
Evocative prose is **the register I default to when I am pleased with an idea.** The better I think the design
is, the more likely I am to describe it beautifully and never state it. **`the_closing_argument` is the craft
I was proudest of yesterday and the least usable thing in the catalog.** That correlation is the warning sign.

## SCOPE OF THE REPAIR
**301 crafts · 859 bounds · every rank grant and intensity line.** This is the largest single authoring debt in
the project and it is entirely mine. **It should be paid down per-file rather than in one pass**, and **new
content must not ship without `plainly` from here on** — a check should enforce presence, since one that
enforced *quality* is not possible.
**CCODE: a ratchet on `plainly` presence for any craft touched after this date would stop the debt growing
while it is being paid.**

## AMENDMENT 1 — IT APPLIES TO MY OWN WRITING, NOT ONLY TO AUTHORED CONTENT
**Ratified same day, because I broke the directive within hours of writing it.**
I specced an arc-standings table with a `how` column and labelled the rows **"by winning", "by holding", "by
standing over", "by turning."** Erik: *"Standing over WHAT? Turning WHAT? Winning — I would guess you mean
battle victories or duel wins."*
**⚠️ THE ORIGINAL DIRECTIVE WAS SCOPED TOO NARROWLY. I wrote it about crafts, bounds and lore — CONTENT — and
then produced jargon in a SPEC, which I had exempted without noticing.**
### THE SHARPER RULE
**A word is jargon when it means something to me because I have been using it all session, and nothing to
anyone else.** These are the highest-risk sites, in order:
1. **⚠️ UI LABELS AND COLUMN HEADERS — worst of all.** No room for context to rescue a bad word.
2. **Spec section names and enum values** — they become code identifiers and then column headers.
3. **My own summary prose to Erik.** If I would have to explain the phrase in the next sentence, it failed.
### THE TEST
**Read the phrase as if you have never seen this project.** *"By standing over"* — over **what**? *"By
turning"* — turning **what**? If the phrase needs the previous six hours of conversation to parse, **it is not
a label, it is a private note.**
### AND THE TELL, SPECIFIC TO ME
**I compress a mechanic I understand well into a short evocative phrase and mistake the compression for
clarity.** *"By turning"* felt precise to me because I know it means *personally pushed an arc to a new
stage.* **Compression is not concision. Concision is short AND complete.**
