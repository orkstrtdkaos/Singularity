## §3 — ⛔ HOLE ONE: THERE IS NO CRAFT FOR LEADING PEOPLE

**Missing across the samurai-ruler and the warlord: `the_gathering` · `raise_banner` · `lead_the_line` ·
`command_field` · `legion_clash`.**

⚠️ **AND THIS IS THE ANSWER TO ERIK'S OWN QUESTION.** He asked: *"Can a player become a samurai and
meaningfully have skills to build and level up to become a high level ruler with that background?"*

⛑ **THE SAMURAI IS FINE. THE RULER IS NOT.** ⚑ **The whole martial half exists** — `edge`, `dressed_edge`,
`perfect_motion`, `held_line`, `read_the_fight`, `disarm` — ⚑ **and so does the honourable half:
`true_account`, `set_word`, `bound_witness`, `broker_truce`, `mediators_tongue`.** ⛔ **What is missing is
EVERYTHING BETWEEN COMMANDING A ROOM AND COMMANDING A FIELD.**

⚠️ **MEASURED: 30 crafts carry `command`, and every one commands A PERSON, A MACHINE OR A SPIRIT** —
`calm_word` (one person), `blazing_word` (a room), `command_engine` (a mechanism), `descent` (a want).
⛔ **NOTHING COMMANDS A BAND.**

⛑ **AND THE ENGINE IS ALREADY WAITING FOR IT:** the standing ladder, `delegationCapacity`, `bandOps`, the
holdings garrison, R46a's `legionClash` — ⚠️ **an entire scale of play with no craft that reaches it.** ⛔ **A
player becomes a leader by ACCUMULATING STANDING, and can never become one by BEING GOOD AT IT.**

⬜ **Proposed — a `command` line, and Aevi would put it in Building/Order rather than Breaking:**

| craft | |
|---|---|
| ⚑ **the gathering** | T2 · `command`, `summon` — ⚠️ **people come when you call, and R46b's temple already uses the word** |
| ⚑ **raise banner** | T3 · `command`, `empower` — ⛔ **a band forms around you and fights better for it** |
| ⚑ **lead the line** | T3 · `command`, `shield` — ⚠️ **you hold a formation together; it breaks when you fall** |
| ⚑ **command field** | T5 · `command`, `foresee` — ⛔ **the capstone: you read a battle and MOVE PEOPLE IN IT** |

## §4 — ⛔ HOLE TWO: THE NECROMANCER CANNOT RAISE ANYTHING

**Missing: `raise_dead` · `command_dead` · `bind_shade`.**

⚠️ **Everything ELSE is there and it is excellent** — `deathsense`, `grey_road`, `hastened_grey`,
`the_attended_end`, `kept_vigil`, `ask_the_dead`, `calling_back`, `given_errand`, and **`set_hand`, which is
authored as *"⚠️ RAISING. The Grave-Callers' craft, and the Palelands have never pretended otherwise."***

⛑ **SO THE RAISING EXISTS — AT EXACTLY ONE CRAFT, WITH NO LADDER UNDER OR OVER IT.** ⛔ **A necromancer can
raise one thing and can never get better at it.**

⚠️ **AND THE BESTIARY ALREADY HAS THE OUTPUT:** `narrowed_dead` is an authored class with a kit, ⛔ **and
nothing a player can cast produces one.**

⬜ **The ladder is small: a T1 that reads the dead, `set_hand` in the middle, a T4 that commands more than
one and a T5 that binds something that ARGUES.** ⚑ **And R29's *"there is still a thread there"* is the
reason the top rung should be dangerous rather than forbidden.**

## §5 — ⚑ AND WHAT THE EXERCISE SAYS ABOUT THE DOMAINS

| domain | crafts | ⬜ |
|---|---|---|
| **Death 44 · Dark 42 · Breaking 39 · Order 38** | ⚑ **rich, and every archetype that draws on them is complete** |
| ⚠️ **Chaos 14 · Spirit 13** | ⛔ **the two thinnest, and the BARBARIAN still built 10/10 from Chaos plus Body** — ⚑ **a thin domain is not automatically a hole; it becomes one when an archetype LIVES there** |
| ⛔ **Building 33** | ⚠️ **carries the artificer completely and the RULER not at all** — ⛑ **the gap is a function family, not a domain** |

⛔ **THE REAL FINDING: THE HOLES ARE BY VERB, NOT BY DOMAIN.** ⚑ **`command` exists 30 times and never at
band scale; `summon` exists and never raises. Both are one function family reaching one scale short.**
