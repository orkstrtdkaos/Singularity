**r1 · Matter Settled** — *fn* `persuade` · *gains* `—`

> ⛔ SET A MATTER OUT TO PEOPLE WILLING TO FOLLOW. A dispute resolves, a council decides, a wrong belief comes apart, and it stays resolved.

**r2 · Inescapable** — *fn* `persuade, bind` · *gains* `targets, conditions`

> ⛔ ADDS COMPULSION AND NUMBERS. Lay premises they must each agree to until they arrive by their own reasoning at a conclusion they would have fled — AND THEY CANNOT ACT AGAINST IT while it holds. Works on a whole body of reasoning minds at once: a council, a court, a company.

> **Cannot:** ⛔ One person for the final form, and it cannot be withdrawn. Everyone who heard it heard it.

**r1 · Named Flaw** — *fn* `hinder, break` · *gains* `—`

> Interrupt an action or claim by naming its actual error out loud — the plan with the gap, the boast with the hole, the ritual with the wrong step. The move falters and must be reworked — the interruption hinders them for as long as the flaw stands unanswered.

> **Cannot:** REACTIVE ONLY — nothing to contradict means nothing happens. The flaw must be REAL; inventing one is Falsecraft and this is not that. The unreasoning are immune.

**r2 · Reductio** — *fn* `hinder, break, bind` · *gains* `targets, conditions`

> Follow their position to where it collapses. A commitment they made becomes a thing they cannot act on — the oath that now forbids the blow, the doctrine that now forbids the door.

> **Cannot:** They must have STATED the position; you cannot bind someone to a belief they never claimed. The shameless simply proceed anyway.

**r3 · Collapse** — *fn* `hinder, break, bind` · *gains* `duration, scope`

> Take a whole structure apart in front of the people who live in it — a case, a command, a faith. What they were doing stops, and it stays stopped.

> **Cannot:** You have made a permanent enemy of everyone whose ground you took, and the truth of it will not soften that. A structure with no flaw stands, and you look like a fool.
