## §2 — ⚑ THE DRAGONS ARE ALREADY RIGHT, AND THE CONCEPT IS THE BEST IN THE BESTIARY

> ⛔ *"A dragon is a domain that **resolved wrong and will not resolve back**. Not an animal grown big: a
> piece of the world's grammar walking, the size of a hill."*

**Three exist, one per extreme:** ⚑ **the Ashen Wyrm** (Pale) · **the Bright Devourer** (Blaze) · **the
Unspooling Worm** (Ruin).

⚠️ **AND THE BESTIARY'S FOUR DESIGN LAWS ARE WHY THEY WORK:**
⛔ *"No person… if it would be a tragedy to kill, it belongs in NPCs, not here."*
⛔ *"A hazard, not a villain — its motivation is at most an instinct or a broken directive, never a
grievance."*
⚑ *"Every entry pressures FUNCTION FAMILIES, not just damage."*
⚑ *"Tied to the world — killing them is also reading the world's temperature."*

⬜ **SO MORE DRAGONS = MORE POLES.** ⚠️ **Twelve axes, three used.** ⛑ **The obvious next three:**
**a LATTICE wyrm** — order that resolved wrong, and it does not fly so much as *arrive already having been
there* · **a ROOTKIN wyrm** — a forest that became one animal and is still growing · ⛔ **a VEIL wyrm — the
absence given wing, and the only one that is not there when you look at it.**

## §3 — ⛔ WIZARDS ARE THE ONE THING THAT SHOULD *NOT* BE ADDED

⚠️ **The game already has 429 crafts across 24 traditions, and *"wizard"* is what every one of them is.**
⛑ **A Cogitant who reached rank 3 in `names_of_power` IS a wizard; the Still Lattice IS an archmage.**

➡️ ⚑ **What is missing is not the archetype, it is THE TOWER: a place wizards are FROM.**
⬜ **The Harmonic Heights and the Radiant Plateau are foothills that teach.** ⛔ **Nothing is a school that
COMPETES** — no rivalry, no lineage feud, no *"which house taught you."* ⚠️ **The Saehara arc has exactly
that (the Still-Water School defending its lineage against a masterless ronin) and it is the strongest
social content in the game.**
