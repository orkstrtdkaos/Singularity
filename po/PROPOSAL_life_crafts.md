## §2 — MUST-HAVE (four)

### ⛔ 1 · SPARK — L5, QUICKENING. The capstone, and the tradition's deepest idea.

**Erik: *"creation is a power NATIVE TO LIFE — it's the spark, the singularity address, not the Wright's
made way."***

⚠️ **JUSTIFIED BY:** the missing capstone · Erik's ruling · and the cosmology. ⛔ **`address` is PRECURSOR
vocabulary in this corpus** (`address_sense`), and **PARAKLETOS DISTRIBUTED ITSELF INTO THE SUBSTRATE —
*"what answers every craft in the world."*** ✅ **If life's creative spark is an address in the substrate,
Life is the tradition holding the evidence, and its capstone should be where a player touches that.**

**What it does:** ⛔ **start something that continues itself.** Not an object — a thing that goes on making
itself. ⚠️ **The hard bound writes itself: you cannot spark what has never lived, and you do not choose
what it becomes.**

### ⛔ 2 · A BEAST CRAFT — L2, FAUNA. The school is named for something it does not do.

⚠️ **JUSTIFIED BY:** ⛔ **`speaking_grove` PROMISES *"plants, ANIMALS, the slow intelligence of old wood"*
AND DELIVERS ONLY WOOD.** The tradition's own craft names the gap.

**What it does:** the working relationship — read a beast, be understood by it, ask rather than command.
⚠️ **NOT a summon and NOT a pet:** `universal_roles` calls the Rootkin expression **the Vivimancer-warden**,
and a warden tends rather than owns.

### ⛔ 3 · AN INFECTION CRAFT — L3, FLORA. The Small, and it takes nothing from Death.

⚠️ **JUSTIFIED BY:** ⛔ **ZERO crafts touch bacteria, ferment, spores or plague** — and Erik's ruling that
Flora is **micro to macro organisms**. ✅ **AND THE TYPE LINE IS CLEAN: decay is the Ashwarden's, but THE
THINGS THAT DO THE DECAYING ARE ALIVE.** `living` is Life's own type and nothing microbial carries it.

**What it does:** ⛔ **end a plague from the inside, or start one.** ⚠️ **A Rootkin who can do this is
frightening rather than pastoral, which the profile demands: *"LIFE AT FLOOD. NOT GENTLE."***

### ⛔ 4 · AN EMPOWER CRAFT — L2, QUICKENING. Erik agreed; `mend` stays out.

⚠️ **JUSTIFIED BY:** the one-line is ***"growth is a force, and a Rootkin can POINT IT"*** — ⛔ **and they
can heal you and cannot make you stronger.** `empower` is absent from a tradition whose entire subject is
life at flood.

## §3 — STRONG (three)

### ⚠️ 5 · A CRAFT FOR THE QUIET CLEARING — L4, and it is the tradition's own horror

⛔ **`cofiring_nodes` authors *"The Quiet in the Quickwood — a single still clearing where THINGS ARE
PERMITTED TO END. Everywhere around it the forest grows so hard IT GROWS OVER YOU; here alone a leaf may
fall and stay fallen."*** ⚠️ **Life 0.9, death 0.8, both blazing.**

**What it does:** ⛔ **make a place where growth stops.** ✅ **This is the tradition's internal check on the
Unceasing made into a craft** — and it is the answer to *"what does a Rootkin do when life will not stop?"*
⚠️ **Possibly a braid with ashwarden; the node is doubly-charged and `skill_functions` already names the
combination *"the rare wielder of both life and death can tell, truly, whether a body should be pulled back
or LET GO."***

### ⚠️ 6 · A GROWN-STRUCTURE CRAFT — L3, FLORA

⛔ **THE HEARTROOT IS A CITY THAT WAS GROWN OVER FOUR HUNDRED YEARS AND THERE IS NO CRAFT FOR IT.**
⚠️ `quicken_the_ground` is `shape: construct` and what it constructs is ACCESS, not a thing. **Nobody grows
a hall.** ✅ **And it is distinct from Building: a Wright makes a thing; this plants something that becomes
one.**

### ⚠️ 7 · A MYCELIAL CRAFT THAT ADMITS WHAT IT IS — L3, FAUNA

⛔ **`green_road` already IS this** — *"the connected life-substrate itself, the road that LINKS EVERY
ROOTED PLACE."* ⚠️ **The tradition has been authoring fungal networks and calling them plants.** ✅ **One
craft that names it — sensing or messaging along the mat — would make FAUNA's mycelial half real rather
than retrofitted.**

## §4 — ⬜ WHAT I WOULD NOT ADD, AND WHY

⛔ **`mend`** — Erik ruled it out. They heal bodies; objects are Building's, and a grove-city that grows its
own halls has few broken things.
⛔ **`soothe`** — ***"NOT GENTLE"*** is the tradition's own line. Their answer to suffering is to make the
body work again.
⛔ **`persuade` · `bargain` · `provoke`** — **Grovehome exists precisely so somebody else does the talking**,
and it *"keeps human hours because trade requires it AND THE ROOTKIN FIND THAT FUNNY."*
⚠️ **`conceal` · `deceive`** — the Eaves are deliberately unmarked and they still let you know you have
arrived. **A people who explain themselves by walking you through an orchard and saying nothing do not
lie — they OUTLAST the question.**
