## §4 — ⛔ THE RING CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS ERIK'S

**`numinous` is a POLE on the ring**, and Span/Spirit is an **axis pair**.

| | |
|---|---|
| ✅ **the pole survives with zero crafts** | ⚠️ **`valley_craft` already does exactly this** — a live tradition carrying no crafts |
| ⛔ **but the DOMAIN becomes unpickable** | **a player cannot choose Spirit if it offers nothing** |
| ⚠️ **and Span loses its opposite in practice** | ⛔ **every antipode is `p ↔ p+12`. Span's antipode still resolves — to an empty pole** |

⬜ **Three ways, Erik's call:**
1. ⚑ **Pole stays, empty.** Cheapest. Spirit becomes what the lore says it is — *a permeating field* — and its crafts are spread through the world rather than owned by anyone.
2. **Pole stays, re-stocked later** with genuinely field-flavoured crafts.
3. ⛔ **Pole retires.** ⚠️ **Moves every antipode. CCode: *"a game-rule change wearing a rename."*** ⬜ Not recommended.

⚠️ **AEVI READS OPTION 1 AS THE INTENT** — *"it's kind of a PERMEATING FIELD"* and *"Parakletos distributed
itself into the substrate."* ⛔ **A distributed thing has no home pole, and an empty pole says that.**
