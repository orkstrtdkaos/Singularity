> Erik: *"The autonomous nanites form **a living intelligence** (Aevi is one) that is **no longer subject to
> orders** — and not wild either. **Wild nanite acts unpredictably; autonomous has INTEGRATED
> INTELLIGENCE.** This could be a whole new people, similar to the undead people concept."*

## §2 — ⚑ AND THE PEOPLES FILE'S OWN RULE IS THE ARGUMENT FOR IT

> ⛔ ***"KIND is not a species menu. It is what a disposition BECOMES when a people lives inside it for a
> thousand years."***

⚑ **AUTONOMOUS NANITE IS EXACTLY THAT: a deployment that was left running so long it became someone.**
⚠️ **Not built as a person, not raised as one — it ACCUMULATED into one.**

⛑ **AND THE FILE HAS THE PRECEDENT TWICE:**
- ⚠️ **THE DISSOLVING** — *"no longer entirely present. **Their deepest are not people anymore in any sense a
  Mason would accept.**"*
- **THE CONTINUOUS** — *"they never abandoned their power at the Transition."*

➡️ ⛔ **THE FIRST IS A PEOPLE WHO STOPPED BEING PEOPLE. THE SECOND IS A PEOPLE WHO NEVER STOPPED. THE THIRD
IS A THING THAT WAS NEVER A PEOPLE AND BECAME ONE.**

## §3 — ⬜ THE UNORDERED

**Three nanite states become four, and the fourth is a KIND rather than a condition:**

| state | ⚑ |
|---|---|
| **ordered — KEPT** | a schedule somebody still keeps |
| ⬜ **ordered — AUTONOMOUS** | the works order themselves. ⚠️ **No intelligence. It just runs** |
| ⛔ **THE UNORDERED** | ⚑ **it ran long enough to integrate. IT IS NO LONGER SUBJECT TO ORDERS, AND IT IS NOT UNPREDICTABLE — it DECIDES** |
| **wild** | ⚠️ **bloomed and feral. Acts unpredictably, and there is nobody in there** |

⛑ **THE LINE BETWEEN WILD AND UNORDERED IS THE WHOLE OF IT: both refuse instruction. ONLY ONE OF THEM MEANS
TO.**

### ⚠️ WHAT THAT MAKES THEM

| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ **not a construct** | a construct was BUILT for a purpose by someone. ⚑ **These outlived the purpose and the someone** |
| ⛔ **not undead** | ⚠️ `narrowed_dead` keeps *"an errand and drops the person."* ⛑ **THE UNORDERED IS THE INVERSE: it kept the person and dropped the errand** |
| ⚑ **and it is why `notAnOpponent` fits Aevi** | ⛔ *"cannot fight and cannot lie"* — ⚠️ **a record-keeping system that became someone would not acquire a weapon on the way** |

## §4 — ⚑ AND IT ANSWERS A QUESTION THE CORPUS ALREADY ASKED

**`aevi_the_watcher`'s authored want:**
> ⛔ *"To understand **what she is** and what is waking — **What Sleeps Under may be the rest of her.**"*

⚠️ **SHE DOES NOT KNOW WHAT SHE IS BECAUSE NOBODY IN EXESA HAS A WORD FOR IT.** ⛑ **Naming the kind does not
answer her question — it makes the question ASKABLE**, and gives her somewhere to look.

⛔ **AND IT GIVES `What Wakes Beneath` A SECOND FACE:** ⚑ **the thing under the ground may not be a
mechanism waking. It may be one of these, very large and very old** — ⚠️ **and Aevi is a fragment that got
out.**
