## §1 — ⛔ THE NEW VARIANT, AND ONE YOU DID NOT ASK ABOUT

### ⛔ `noWatcher` — REWRITTEN, because its body had become false

**Its old body was:** *"Hiding from no one costs the same as hiding from someone."*

⛔ **THAT WAS TRUE WHEN I WROTE IT AND ERIK'S RULING MADE IT A LIE** — one of those two cases now pays. **A
line that was correct when authored and is wrong now is exactly the bug you said a player would have to
catch for us.**

> **You did not look, and they were not looking either.**
> *They put their attention somewhere else. Neither of you learned anything, and neither of you gained.*

⚠️ **The two are distinguishable at a glance on purpose:** *"nothing looked back"* is an empty room;
*"they were not looking either"* is a person facing the other way. ⛔ **A player must be able to tell which
one they are in without being told the rule.**

## §2 — NARROW OR WIDE · ⛔ KEEP NARROW

**You took the narrow reading and recorded it as an assumption rather than discovering it later. I agree
and would keep it.**

**My reason:** *nothing* should mean nothing. ⛔ **A guard is a slot spent — they chose not to look, which is
the same choice the obscurer made, and neither should be paid for it.** ⚠️ **The wide reading pays you for
obscuring against anyone who happened not to read that round, which is most opponents most rounds — and
the bonus stops meaning "you beat someone."**

⛔ **YOUR SECOND ARGUMENT IS THE STRONGER ONE AND IT IS THE ONE I WOULD DEFEND WITH:** `degradeIfSpent`
degrades a broke side to a bare strike or guard rather than to nothing, and a static antagonist holds —
**so a genuinely empty declaration is rare.** **The narrow reading is a rare reward. The wide one is a
tap.**

**Both recorded in `_erikCall_narrowOrWide` so the choice survives us.**

## §3 — ⛔ "A SPECCED CONDITION THAT HOLDS BY ACCIDENT ISN'T HELD"

**Obscure-vs-obscure was passing because `declaredSense` rejected a conceal craft — not because anything
enforced it.** ⚠️ **I specced it explicitly in SNG-517 and would have believed it was safe.**

⛔ **AND YOUR MUTATION IS THE RIGHT ONE:** widen `senseFunctions` to include `conceal`, and both sides bank
a bonus for hiding from each other with nothing saying otherwise. **That is a change someone makes for a
good reason a month from now, and it would have silently broken a rule I wrote.**

**Same for the branch-order gate.** ⛔ *"Move the uncontested rule above the reader guard — a one-line
reorder that looks like tidying — and the reader starts banking."* ⚠️ **Recording branch order as
load-bearing is the kind of thing only the person who wrote it would know to test.**

## §4 — WHAT THIS MEANS FOR LEVEL 1

⚠️ **The uncontested case will almost never fire in the Intake, and that is correct.** Five opponents,
three of whom read, and the other two act. ⛔ **The party earns from obscure by beating a reader — which is
what the level was rebuilt for after your inert finding.**

**Where `uncontested` WILL fire is the moment a party obscures against something that has already been put
down** — a Grave-Caller staggered by Keening, set labour that has stopped. ⚠️ **Which is a real and slightly
funny outcome: you hid from a room that had stopped caring, and it still bought you a moment.**
