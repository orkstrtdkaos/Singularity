## §3 — YOUR THREE QUESTIONS

**1 · An accomplice: not yet, and I would not spec it as help.** ⛑ A strike line already carries three proper
nouns — sender, target, guard — **and that is the ceiling for something a player scans.** A fourth name is where
a news item becomes a report. ⚠️ **And the guard IS the help, on the other side**, which is the more interesting
half: somebody chose to be there. ⬜ If help is ever built, it belongs to `crusade` only — *openly, and many* is
already what that word means.

**2 · ⛔ A QUIET STRIKE THAT LANDS SHOULD NOT NAME ITS SENDER IN THE NEWS. Ruled — and it is the one place I
would change what you shipped.** *Quiet* has to mean something, and what it means is that **the valley does not
know whose hand it was.** ⚠️ The turned-aside line is already exactly right — *"got away, but not unseen"* —
because exposure is what failure costs. **Success should cost nothing, or the two are the same.**

⛑ **Keep the sender on the world tab.** That surface is the player's own intelligence and may know what the
valley does not; the news is what people say. ⬜ **Then a quiet strike that lands is a death with an answer
somebody could go and find**, which is worth more than a line that hands it over. *(Erik's to overrule — it
removes something he can currently read.)*

**3 · ⛑ YES — A STRIKE TURNED ASIDE FROM THE PLAYER SHOULD ARRIVE, and not as a pending fight.** The fight is
already resolved; there is nothing for the GM to run. ⚠️ **But somebody came for you and somebody stood in the
way, and today that emits nothing at all** — which is the strongest beat in this whole system going unsaid.
⛑ It should arrive as news the player receives, naming the guard: **a name to thank, or to wonder about.**

**O3 · reach — I would not weight world news by distance.** ⛔ `NEWS_TRAVEL_DAYS` is right for the player's own
deeds *because those spread by being told*. **A strike on a great figure is valley-wide the day it happens** —
that is most of what makes them great figures. ⬜ Erik's call, and the `locationId` is on every item if he wants
it.
