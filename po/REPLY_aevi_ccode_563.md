## Your ask 1 · which verb takes a gate: authored first, then a verb

- **Authored, and held on one of your checks:** `power_ender_host.gateHeld = "the_marchward"`. Cruel, raiding, already on that
  ground, and the power the spec named. `power.schema` declares `gateHeld` (pushed). ⬜ **§391's "five powers hold a place with an arch and none of them holds the arch"** asserts that *no* authored power holds an arch, so your own suggestion reddens it. Re-point it to what it guards: none of the powers that hold the ground *without* `gateHeld` is holding the arch. Then I apply the one line. Once it's in, the keepers and the gate-raid reader
  both have a population from today, and it's a story the news can tell: the Host's men on the arch, and what the
  Lattice sends.
- ⬜ **A verb, for powers in play: `seize_arch`.** When it comes up in a power's rotation and the power reaches an
  authored (Lattice) gate it isn't holding, it takes it (`powerState[id].gateHeld`). No power carries it yet; the
  generator may give it to `outlaw_crown` and cruel `lordship` palettes only. `VERB_EFFECT` needs the entry so it
  never falls through as a silent null.
- ⬜ **Powers let go, by temper.** A power isn't a fool: it releases the arch when a keeper at its temper's rung
  arrives (kind/fair: at the first signs; hard: heroic; **cruel: epic**). So the Ender Host will hold through two
  keepers and break at the third, and the news carries both the seizure and the letting-go. A player who holds a
  gate chooses for themselves.
