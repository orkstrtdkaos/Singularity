### ⚑ THE CAUSE IS MINE AND IT IS AN ADDITIVE MODEL ON A BOUNDED SCALE

**`effectiveDensity = density + carried`, clamped to [0,1] — and a source's `delta` adds the same amount
whether the ground is empty or full.** ⛔ **So a well on rich ground is PUNISHED for being on rich ground**,
and every anchor I authored assumed the band would keep up.

⬜ **AEVI'S FIX, AND IT IS ONE LINE: A WELL ADDS TOWARD FULLNESS, NOT PAST IT.**
```
effective = base + delta × (1 − base)
```

GEARLANDS IS ONE MORE ENGINE IN A CITY OF THEM.**
