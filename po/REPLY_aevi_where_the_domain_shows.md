## §1 — ⛔ THE ANSWER FOLLOWS FROM READING B, AND IT IS RESTRAINT

**Under Reading B the pole is still the identity.** A Cogitant is a Cogitant. ⛔ **So the domain must NOT
appear anywhere a player identifies themselves**, or we have quietly re-run Reading A in the interface.

⚠️ **THE TEST FOR EVERY SURFACE: does the player need the domain to make THIS decision?** If not, showing it
is noise that teaches a wrong model.

---

## §2 — WHERE IT SHOWS

### ✅ 1 · THE LEARN SCREEN — **yes, and this is the one that matters**

⛔ **Because it is where access is DECIDED, and access is the only place the domain does real work.** A
player choosing a secondary needs to see that `figurist` sits with `cogitant`, because that is what makes
the choice legible instead of arbitrary.

✅ **AS A GROUPING, NOT A LABEL:** poles grouped under a domain heading. **The thing you pick is still the
pole.**

### ✅ 2 · THE SKILL GRAPH — **yes, as a layer that can be turned off**

⚠️ The graph is where 24 traditions are genuinely hard to hold in your head. ⛔ **But the ring is still the
ring** — 24 positions, real distances, real antipodes — **so the domain is a lens over it, not a
replacement for it.** ✅ **Default the lens ON for new players and let it be dismissed.**

### ⛔ 3 · THE CHARACTER SHEET — **NO. This is the one I would refuse.**

**The sheet says who you are.** ⛔ **You are a Cogitant.** ⚠️ Putting "Mind" on the sheet is exactly the
move that makes players think their tradition is Mind — **and Erik's ruling was that it is not.**

⬜ **ONE EXCEPTION WORTH BUILDING:** if a character holds poles from **more than one** domain, the sheet
could say so, because that is a real fact about them and the domain is the only way to express it briefly.

### ⚠️ 4 · ABILITY CARDS — **no, with one exception**

**A card should say the craft's tradition.** ⛔ Adding a domain line to 412 cards is 412 places to maintain
a derived value, and the player reading a card is not making an access decision.

✅ **THE EXCEPTION IS THE ANTIPODE CRAFT** — now learnable-but-not-castable. ⛔ **That card MUST say so
loudly**, and it is the one card where the domain relationship is the whole point: *"Umbracraft — braid
material only. You cannot cast this."*

## §3 — ✅ AND ONE PLACE YOU DID NOT LIST, WHICH I THINK IS THE REAL WIN

⛔ **CHARACTER CREATION.** Twenty-four traditions is a wall at the moment of first contact, and it is the
only moment where a player has no context at all. ✅ **Fourteen domains, each opening into its poles, is a
genuinely better first screen** — and it costs nothing structurally, because the thing they end up choosing
is still a pole.

⚠️ **That is the merger's actual payoff for a player**, and it is worth more than any of the four surfaces
you listed.
