## §3 — TWO RULINGS I NEED

### §3a — ⛔ WHAT IS THE HARMONIC HEIGHTS A FOOTHILL *OF*?

**`radiant_folk` is easy: Radiance is the Blazeborn craft**, and the Plateau folk do a gentler, more useful
version of it — Sun Coax, Light Well, Clarity Lens rather than revelation-as-violence. **Foothill of
blazeborn.**

⚠️ **Harmonic has no obvious parent, because SOUND IS NOT ONE OF THE TWELVE AXES.** Its abilities are
Sonic Resonance, Echo Sense, Resonant Shield, Chord of Mending, Voice of the Flock.

**Two candidates:**
- **threnodist** (Pathos, emotional) — **both work in sound**, the Threnodists in lament and the Harmonics
  in resonance. *"Tidal, expressive, public weeping"* against *standing waves*. ⚠️ The Threnodists sing
  what they FEEL; the Heights sing what they can BUILD WITH.
- **lattice** (Latticework, order) — a standing wave is structure imposed on air, and the Heights are a
  built civilisation of phononic crystal.

⛔ **I lean threnodist on craft and lattice on aesthetic, which is exactly the kind of split I should not
resolve alone.**

### §3b — Are there other foothills I have not spotted?

**If Harmonic and Radiant are foothills because they are the folk-scale version at a specific place, then
the same question applies to `valley_craft`'s sixteen** — is Rivercraft a Horizon foothill, is Greenlore a
Rootkin one? ⚠️ **Or is "Folk" a different category from "foothill" entirely:** foothill = a lesser
expression of ONE parent, folk = the ordinary-person version of MANY.

⛔ **I think that distinction is the real content of your correction and I want it stated before I re-file
anything.**
