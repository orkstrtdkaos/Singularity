## §2 — ⛔ WHY THIS IS BETTER THAN WHAT IT REPLACES

**The closure was the only gate in the model that was not a cost.** Every other tier is a price — capstones
barred to adjacents, tier caps on secondary and tertiary, a skill-point penalty elsewhere. ⚠️ **The antipode
alone was a wall**, and it produced three bad results:

1. ⛔ **`Span` could never be held whole** — your §2. It is the one domain holding both poles of an axis, and
   its two sects were permanently barred from each other.
2. ⛔ **The exception carried all the weight.** Braids were *"the ONLY sanctioned road to your own
   antipode"* — and **three braids are authored against twelve axes.** ⚠️ **Nine axes had a wall and no
   door.**
3. ⚠️ **It closed the most interesting character in the game:** the Blazeborn who has been to the Umbral
   Depths and come back changed.

✅ **AND THE DESIGN NOTE'S POINT SURVIVES WORD FOR WORD.** It says *"holding an axis WHOLE is forbidden to
you by ordinary means and reachable only by braiding."* ⛔ **Still true.** You can learn it and you still
cannot use it. **The braid remains the only way to spend what you learned.**

⚠️ **AND BRAIDS GET STRONGER, NOT WEAKER.** A braid is now **the thing that turns dead knowledge into a
craft** — which is a better reason to carry one than *"it is the only way."*
