## R29 ✅ RULED — attending decides how much person survives

§48.3 lists four candidates for what decides **STABLE (Afterling)** vs **NARROWING (unminded purpose)**:
*the depth raised from · whether they were GIVEN a purpose or merely set · **whether anyone attended
them** · whether a name was kept.*

✅ **Erik: *"I like the idea that attending does keep one a person more completely upon revival or
reanimation."***

⚠️ **NOTE THE PHRASING — it is a GRADIENT, not a switch.** *"More completely"*, not *"stable versus
narrowed."* ⛔ **Attending is not a binary that decides which end state; it decides HOW MUCH PERSON
SURVIVES THE CROSSING.** The other three candidates stay live and may still contribute.

### ⛔ WHAT THIS RETROACTIVELY EXPLAINS

**The entire Ashwarden discipline.** Attending an ending is not ceremony, sentiment, or respect for the
dead. ➡️ **It is the mechanism that preserves personhood across the crossing.** A tradition of people who
sit with the dying because the alternative is that what comes back is less of someone.

✅ **`the_attended_end` becomes the most load-bearing craft in the Death domain** — and it was already
authored as though it knew that.

⬜ **Applies to revival and reanimation both** — Erik's wording covers `calling_back` retrieval as well as
raising.
