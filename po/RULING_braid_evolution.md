## R39a — a minted braid emits ONE rank ✅ RULED

| | |
|---|---|
| **a minted braid is not incomplete** | ⚑ **it is UNDECIDED** |

➡️ ⛔ **THE "DOWNGRADE" OBJECTION DISSOLVES.** You do not receive a smaller craft — **you receive one that
is still becoming, and your use of it is the authorship.**

## ⬜ THE SHAPE, for CCode to price

1. **Mint at rank 1**, `wantsAuthoring: true`, ⛔ **no rank 2 or 3 authored at all.**
2. **Accrue.** `uses` counts; `coActivations` records the company it kept; ⬜ intensity, stretch and
   target-kind are also knowable at cast time.
3. **At the rank-2 threshold the braid NOMINATES ITSELF** — ⚠️ **with evidence.** *"Cast 22 times, 17 of
   them alongside `palework`, mostly at surge, mostly against structures."*
4. **Rank 2 is authored FROM that evidence.** ⬜ **By whom is the open question — see below.**
5. **Repeat for rank 3.**

### ⛔ AND IT CLOSES THE PIPELINE LOOP PROPERLY

`SPEC_generative_pipeline.md` §3 names `wantsAuthoring` as the nomination queue. ⚠️ **Under R39b a
nomination arrives with a USE HISTORY attached** — ➡️ **which is the difference between *"someone made a
thing"* and *"here is what it has been for."***

## ⚠️ NO MIGRATION NEEDED

**Six live saves hold three-rank braids.** ⛔ **They stay valid.** ➡️ **Three-rank braids simply stop being
minted.** ⚠️ **An existing braid is a craft that was decided for you; a new one is a craft you decide.**

## ⬜ OPEN

1. ⛔ **WHO AUTHORS RANK 2?** ⚠️ Three candidates and Aevi will not guess: **(a)** the engine picks from a
   shaped set based on the use pattern; **(b)** it goes to Aevi's queue and a human writes it; **(c)** the
   GM narrates and the engine adopts. ➡️ ⚠️ **(b) gives the best prose and is slowest; (a) is immediate and
   generic — which is the defect this ruling exists to kill.**
2. **What dimensions of use are read?** ⬜ `coActivations` certainly. ⚠️ Intensity, stretch, target kind and
   whether it harmed are all knowable — **which ones MATTER is a design call.**
3. **Does the same apply to bond-taught crafts?** ⚠️ `marrow-s-wings` was taught, not braided.
4. ⬜ **Can a braid evolve WRONGLY** — used carelessly for 20 castings and rank 2 is worse than it might
   have been? ⚠️ **Aevi's instinct: yes, and that is the point.** ⛔ **Erik's call.**
