## R37b — ⛔ THE THREE TERMS STACK ✅ RULED

⚠️ **AEVI HAD PROPOSED "HIGHEST WINS" AND CHANGED HER MIND BEFORE THE RULING. THE REVERSAL IS THE POINT:**

| term | is |
|---|---|
| **acquaintance** | ⚑ being **WITH** you |
| **charge** | ⚑ holding **YOUR** work |
| **deeds** | ⚑ doing things **IN THE WORLD** |

⛔ **THESE ARE THREE GENUINELY DIFFERENT KINDS OF WORK.** ➡️ **Someone doing all three IS advancing faster
than someone doing one, and the model should say so.**

✅ **AND THE RUNAWAY RISK IS ALREADY CONTAINED: the deed gates are steep — 170 deeds for the top tier.**
⚠️ **Stacking cannot outrun a curve that shallow at the top.**

## R37c — ⬜ THE SERVICE BAND STAYS UNSET ✅ RULED

**Aevi had guessed N = 5.** ⛔ **Withdrawn — because career → tier → level is already live, a delegate
accruing deeds is promoted by the same gates as anybody else, so the gap closes on its own.**

⚠️ **A band would only ever catch ONE case: a steward with a charge and NO deeds** — someone who genuinely
does nothing but hold.

➡️ ✅ **LEAVE IT UNSET. Watch whether the deed gates close the gap in play. Add the floor only if a
delegate still ends up fragile when the player returns.**

⛔ **THE PRINCIPLE: do not author a safety net for a failure the existing curve may not produce.** ⚠️ **An
unneeded floor is free levelling with a justification.**
