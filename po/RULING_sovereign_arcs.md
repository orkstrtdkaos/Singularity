## R40a — a fed Sovereign is a BENEFICIARY, not the engine ✅ RULED

⛔ **Arcs keep their own causes.** The Lattice orders more because that is what Latticework does. The
Cogitarium abandons bodies because Cogitants reason their way out of the flesh. ⚠️ **None of it happens
BECAUSE a Sovereign wills it.**

➡️ ✅ **THE SOVEREIGNS ARE VERY GOOD AT PROFITING FROM IT.** `arc_the_poles_pull` stage 1 already reads
*"the Lattice orders more, the Blaze burns brighter, **the Cogitarium abandons more bodies**, the Pale wins
ground from the Quick"* — ⚑ **authored as world-drift, and it IS a supply line. Nobody wrote it that way.**

⚠️ **WHY NOT THE STRONGER VERSION:** a world where every problem traces to seven entities is **smaller**
than one where they merely feed on what was going to happen anyway. ⛔ **And Erik's own frame holds:
*"this can be a super arc but it doesn't have to be the whole spine."***

## R40b — ⛔ BUT AN ARRIVAL PUSHES THE ARC DIRECTLY ✅ RULED

> Erik: *"agreed, but some of them show up when you're successful at starving them — that **pushes
> (attempts)** the arc directly."*

⚑ **THE ARRIVAL IS THE EXCEPTION, AND IT IS THE ONLY ONE.**

| state | relation to the arc |
|---|---|
| **fed** | ⬜ beneficiary. Takes what the drift produces. **Pushes nothing** |
| **losing supply** | agents redirected — ⚠️ **their deeds may push, as any figure's do** |
| **campaigning** | organised opposition, still indirect |
| ⛔ **ARRIVED** | ⚑ **A DIRECT ARC PUSH** |

⚠️ **NOTE THE WORD: *ATTEMPTS*.** ⛔ **An arrival pushes; it does not decide.** ✅ **`arcContests` and
`arcTurnings` already exist** — 14 turnings recorded in Silas's save, each naming who turned it — ➡️ **so a
Sovereign's push is contested by whoever is standing there, like any other.**

### ⚑ AND THE LOOP CLOSES ON ITSELF

**Starving a Sovereign is how you stop it feeding on an arc. ⛔ SUCCEEDING IS WHAT BRINGS IT TO PUSH THAT
ARC IN PERSON.**

➡️ ⚠️ **The interdiction and the crisis are the same act at different depths** — which is R29's shape
again, and Erik's own line: *"you are the reason I am hungry, and you are therefore the only thing that can
finish me."*

⛔ **AND IT ARRIVES DIMINISHED**, because arriving is diminishment — it must accept a shape to act here.
⚠️ **So the push is real and resistible, which is exactly what an `arcContest` is for.**

## ⬜ WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE BUILD

1. ⛔ **NO Sovereign term in ordinary arc advancement.** ⚠️ Stages advance from figures and drift, as today.
2. **A Sovereign's supply state is DERIVED from its arc's stage**, not the other way round — ⬜ a foreclosing
   axis feeds its Sovereign.
3. ⚑ **An arrival is an `arcContest` entry with a Sovereign as the pusher** — ⚠️ **the only path by which
   one touches an arc directly.**
4. ⬜ **Agents push as ORDINARY FIGURES.** ⚠️ They are *"not possessed — CORRECT"*, so their deeds land in
   `figureCareer` like anyone's. ⛔ **Nothing marks them as an agent in the data, and that is the point.**
