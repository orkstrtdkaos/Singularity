## R41b — ⚑ THREE ENDINGS, AND THE THIRD IS THE INTERESTING ONE ✅ RULED

> Erik: *"where they are either **beaten and slain**, or they **manage to push the arc back to the point
> where they recross the veil.**"*

| ending | |
|---|---|
| ⚑ **SLAIN** | it is finished. ⚠️ **An axis has no claimant, and that is its own consequence** |
| ⚑ **YOU FAIL** | the arc forecloses. ⛔ *"a scatter of pure, deadly poles with less and less between them"* |
| ⛔ **IT RECROSSES** | ⚠️ **it pushes the arc back far enough to be FED AGAIN, and leaves** |

➡️ ⛔ **THE THIRD IS NOT A DRAW.** ⚠️ **It got what it needed and went home.** ✅ **The player survives, the
world is worse, and the thing that came is still out there fed.** ⚑ **Nobody writes that ending, and it is
the honest one for a creature that never wanted to be here.**

⚠️ **AND IT FOLLOWS FROM R40b's WORD — *ATTEMPTS*.** An arrival PUSHES the arc; ⛔ **if the push succeeds it
has restored its own supply and has no reason to stay.**
