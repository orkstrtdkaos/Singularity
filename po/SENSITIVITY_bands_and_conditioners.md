## §5 — ⚑ AND THE ITEMS ARE THE REASON THIS DOES NOT HAVE TO BE PERFECT

> Erik: *"We don't have to get this perfect because **we have items and artifacts that can carry power
> sources and be a local sink or well** — we should probably come up with **items that are power
> conditioners or transformers** too."*

⛑ **THAT CHANGES WHAT TUNING IS FOR. The bands set the WORLD'S SHAPE; items are how a player ANSWERS it.**

| ⬜ kind | ⚑ what it does |
|---|---|
| ⚑ **a CARRIED WELL** | a local pool — ⚠️ **the same `substrateSource` shape the world already uses, on a thing you can put down** |
| ⚑ **a CARRIED SINK** | ⛔ **thins the lattice around it — which is to say IT OPENS A DOOR**, and that should frighten somebody |
| ⛑ **a CONDITIONER** | ⚠️ **narrows the penalty without moving the ground: it does not make the place denser, it makes you less sensitive to it** |
| ⛔ **a TRANSFORMER** | ⚑ **reads one source and presents another** — ⚠️ **stand in ordered nanite, cast metaphysical.** The single most interesting item in the list and the most dangerous to balance |

⛔ **AND THE CORPUS ALREADY HAS THE PRECEDENT:** ⚠️ **`carriedSubstrate` is an authored key in
`the_substrate.json`** — ⛑ **carrying power is a solved idea; carrying a FIELD is the new part.**

⬜ **Design rules Aevi would hold to:**
1. ⚠️ **A conditioner narrows a penalty. It never grants a source you do not have** — ⛔ that is a
   transformer, and transformers should be rare, large, and probably Precursor.
2. ⛑ **A carried sink is a weapon and an invitation.** ⚠️ **Thinning the divide is what a Sovereign wants
   done.**
3. ⛔ **Nothing carried should beat standing in the right place.** ⚑ **An item is how you work where you
   are, not a reason to stop caring where you are** — ⚠️ **otherwise the whole field model stops mattering
   and the ground card has nothing to say.**
