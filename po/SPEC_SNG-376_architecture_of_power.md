## §2 — ⛔ THE TEST FOR WHETHER SOMETHING IS A SOURCE

Before adding more, the discriminator — otherwise "what else?" produces a list that grows forever:

> **A source is real if it has its OWN answer to: what does thin lattice ground do to it?**
> Precursor: penalised. Natural: improved (SNG-172 §3.1, Erik-ratified). Wild: distorted, both crit
> bands widen.
> ⚠️ **If a candidate's answer is identical to an existing source's, it is FLAVOUR, not a source.**

**This makes the taxonomy checkable rather than a matter of taste, and it ties directly to SNG-172's
invariant 2: every region must be SOMEONE'S good ground.** Each source is, in effect, a different map of
where the world is kind to you.

## §3 — THE FIVE, AS OUTCOMES

| source | what it draws on | thin ground does | its cost | its failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Precursor lattice** | the alien substrate that reads structured will | ⛔ **penalises** — nothing to speak to | energy; the lattice answers, you do not push | it stops answering |
| **Pre-transition nanite** | deployed human machinery, still running, still obeying its old spec | ⚠️ **indifferent to depth, sensitive to CONDITION** — works where the machines are maintained | maintenance, supply, expertise | it breaks, and breakage is mechanical and repairable |
| **Wild nanite** | the tangled, untended field | ⚠️ **distorts** — both crit bands widen (SNG-140) | unpredictability, and it is not free | it does something else |
| **Metaphysical** | mind reaching past matter, opened by the Transition | ⚠️ **indifferent to lattice; sensitive to the PRACTITIONER** | attention, self, coherence | it reaches back |
| **Body & technique** | trained flesh, breath, hand | ✅ **improved** (SNG-172 §3.1) — thin ground is quiet ground | conditioning, injury, age | you are simply not good enough today |

**⛔ COMBINATION IS A MODE, NOT A SIXTH SOURCE.** SNG-172 invariant 3: a combination ability scales by its
MIX, never rounded to a dominant label. ⚠️ **Erik's own Cogitant example is the case: nanite to move the
cup, metaphysics for the mind that aims it — and thin ground should help one half and hurt the other in
the same action.** Making combination a source would destroy exactly the effect it exists to produce.

## §4 — THREE CANDIDATES. Each passes or fails §2's test, and I do not think they are equal.

**A · BELIEF / CONSENT — I think this IS a source.** The God-Named's borrowed name *"works only on those
who credit it."* The Bargainers' term requires acceptance; **refusal ends it completely.** ⚠️ **Its answer
to thin ground is genuinely its own: lattice density is IRRELEVANT and POPULATION is everything.** A
God-Named is powerful in a crowded believing town and helpless alone on a mountain — **the exact inverse
of a Rootkin.** That is a new map, which is what §2 asks for.

**B · THE OTHER SIDE — I think this IS a source, and it is the uncomfortable one.** The Numinous thin the
veil and *something reaches through*. The Abyssal Choir introduces a hunger and *it* does the eating.
⚠️ **Its answer to thin ground is inverted: the veil is THINNER where the lattice is, so the Numinous get
stronger exactly where a Rootkin starves.** ⛔ **And it has a cost structure nothing else has — the power
is not yours and it has its own intentions.** Every other source fails by not working; this one fails by
working and then continuing.

**C · THE DEAD / WHAT IS REMEMBERED — I do NOT think this is a source, and I want to be argued with.**
Threnodists carry names; `ask_the_dead` exists. ⚠️ **But its answer to thin ground looks identical to
metaphysical's, which by §2 makes it flavour.** **Unless the dead are literally persistent in the lattice
— in which case it is Precursor with a very particular idiom, and that is a fiction decision, not a
taxonomy one.** Erik's call and it is a real fork.

## §5 — WHAT I WOULD ASK BEFORE THE TRADITION MAP

1. ⛔ **Are pre-transition nanite and Precursor lattice really different, or is one a subset?** The whole
   split rests on there being human machinery that predates the Transition and postdates the Precursors.
   **If that is true it is a large piece of setting and worth stating once, properly.**
2. **Belief and the Other Side — in or out?** §4 argues both in. **If in, we go from five to seven and
   that is the ceiling I would defend.**
3. ⚠️ **Does a tradition have ONE source or a mix?** Erik's own framing says mix — *"the Cogitants have
   harnessed metaphysics as well as just masterful thought"*, *"Seraphs and Abyssals are mostly nanite."*
   **"Mostly" is the operative word: I would author a WEIGHTED mix per tradition, not a label**, so
   `seraphic: {precursor 0.6, belief 0.3, metaphysical 0.1}` — and an ability inherits its tradition's mix
   unless it declares its own.
4. ⛔ **The lore file must be corrected either way.** *"All power in this world is nanite-mediated"* has
   been false since Erik ratified SNG-172 in July, and every ability authored since has been written
   against a sentence that was already overturned.
