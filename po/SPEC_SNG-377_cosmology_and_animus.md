## §1 — WHAT ERIK ESTABLISHED (verbatim in substance, so it is not paraphrased away)

- **The Precursors are the entities Genesis describes** — Father, Son, Holy Spirit, the angels. Very
  powerful aliens who harnessed enough power and technology to be immortal and deeply knowledgeable.
  **They seeded life on Earth and built the systems that made it habitable. Then they disappeared.**
- **What is beyond the Veil is Lucifer and the demons** — roughly the ones the Precursors are fleeing
  from, or battling, **across time and distance scales long enough that a human span is not the unit.**
- **The Seraphs are a new generation — human, but evolved — on the Precursor side.**
- **The Abyssal Choir stands on the opposite side.**
- **Lattice and nanite are SEPARATE.** Ratified.

## §2 — ⛔ FOUR CONSEQUENCES THAT FALL OUT IMMEDIATELY, and they are large

**§2a — THE LATTICE IS WAR INFRASTRUCTURE.** It is not neutral physics. It was built by one side of a
conflict that has not ended. ⚠️ **Every lattice-user in the valley is drawing on a belligerent's works,
whether or not they know it** — an Enginewright tuning a mechanism and a Seraphic passing judgment are
using the same side's equipment. **That is a fact about the world, not a moral claim about them.**

**§2b — THE VEIL IS A DEFENSIVE WORK, NOT A NATURAL BOUNDARY.** ⛔ **This reclassifies the Numinous
entirely.** They are not poking holes in a curtain — **they are opening a breach in a wall that was put
there on purpose, by the side that built everything else.** `the_thinned_veil` already says *"it does not
leave when you are finished"* and *"something on the other side may notice the opening."* **Under this
canon those lines stop being flavour and become strategic facts.**

⚠️ **And it explains the source model without inventing anything: Precursor and Veil are opposed because
they are two sides of a war, and the Veil is thin where the wall is weak.**

**§2c — THE DEAD ARE CONTESTED MATERIEL.** A saved pattern is something both sides can use — the lattice
archives it and can rewrite it onto a new body; the far side can pull a presence back across. ⛔ **A dead
person is therefore not merely mourned. They are a resource in a war**, and that is why attendance
matters.

**§2d — SERAPHIC AND ABYSSAL ARE NOT A PHILOSOPHICAL AXIS. THEY ARE TWO DEVOTIONS IN A REAL CONFLICT.**
⚠️ **They may still sit on the `demonic_angelic` reach mechanically, but the reach is a description of
their craft, not of what they are.** Erik should confirm whether the reach axis IS the war axis or merely
runs parallel to it — **it changes whether a cross-pole braid between them is a technique or a treason.**

## §3 — ⛔ THE ASHWARDENS, RE-FOUNDED (Erik's, and it is the best thing in this session)

Erik: *"An Ashwarden attends a death… because they know how many things out there might want to
capitalize on that dead soul/presence/pattern, and they guard it so it can die on its natural path."*

**This gives the tradition a REASON where it previously had only an aesthetic.** They were "people who
tend endings," which is atmosphere. **They are now a guard detail in a war most of the valley does not
know is happening**, and every one of their existing crafts re-reads correctly under it:

| craft | old reading | ⛔ under §3 |
|---|---|---|
| `deathsense` | feel where something is dying | **know where a pattern is about to become available** |
| `the_kept_vigil` | hold a failing thing at its edge | **hold the line while it goes the natural way** |
| `the_attended_end` (Marrow's) | tell you plainly what is ending | **witness, so the ending is not unobserved** |
| Marrow's vow — *"will not hasten an ending, ever, for any reason, including mercy"* | a bird's scruple | ⛔ **a sentry's standing order.** Hastening creates an opportunity. |

⚠️ **NOT ALL OF THEM**, per Erik — this is a reason available to the tradition, not a uniform doctrine.
**The ones who hold it are the ones with the animus.**

## §4 — ⛔ THE ANIMUS LAYER. Erik: *"we need to start intentionally authoring animus into the systems."*

**Measured: traditions carry `opposite` and `cultOfPurity` and NOTHING ELSE relational.** Figures have
`rivals` (58 of 66). **Traditions have no enemies.**

**⛔ AND `opposite` IS NOT AN ENEMY — that is the whole point and the reason a new field is needed.**

| relation | example | feeling |
|---|---|---|
| **`opposite`** — axis antipode | rootkin ↔ ashwarden (growth ↔ endings) | ⚠️ **mutual respect, usually.** Each is what the other is not, and both know the other is necessary. |
| **`cultOfPurity`** — their own extremist wing | the Unceasing, the Hollow, the Unfallen | shame, fear, *"that is what we become if we stop checking ourselves"* |
| **⛔ `animus` — NEW** | **ashwarden → abyssal** | **hatred, and it is EARNED.** Not an axis. A grievance. |

**Erik's worked case: an Ashwarden hates the Abyssals who prey on entities to feed the other side.**
⚠️ **Note that ashwarden and abyssal are not opposites on any axis** — which is exactly why the existing
fields could not express it. **Animus is orthogonal to the great circle.**

### §4a — What an `animus` entry needs

Not a list of enemies. **A grievance has a shape:**

`{ toward, because, expressedAs, whatWouldEndIt }`

⚠️ **`whatWouldEndIt` is the field that keeps this from being a hate-list** — a grievance that cannot be
resolved is a faction tag, and this world already has enough of those. **Some should be genuinely
irreconcilable and that should be rare and deliberate.**

### §4b — What it is FOR, mechanically and at the table

⛔ **Do not build a reputation modifier and call it done.** Animus should show up as:
- **NPC disposition** — an Ashwarden NPC meeting an Abyssal player character has a starting position
- **What a tradition will and will not teach you**, and to whom
- ⚠️ **What a braid across that line COSTS** — see §2d; a Seraphic/Abyssal braid may be a technique to a
  scholar and a betrayal to both traditions
- **Quest and arc hooks that write themselves** once a tradition wants something from another
