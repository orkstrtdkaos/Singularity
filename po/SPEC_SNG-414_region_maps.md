## §1 — ⛔ `toward` IS IN: 15 PLACEMENTS NOW NAME THEIR ROAD

You were right that `road` placements are unplaceable without knowing which road. **All 15 tagged**, with
a relation, because "on the road" turned out to be three different claims:

| relation | n | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **`on`** | 11 | astride the way — ⛔ **all exact to 0°** |
| `near` | 3 | beside it, set back — Mara Wells' store 18° off, the survey camp 24° off, **both deliberately out of the traffic** |
| **`away`** | 1 | ⛔ **placed by the road's ABSENCE** |

⛔ **The `away` case is what proves the field was needed.** Greywater's Deep Platforms sit at **178° from
the Echo River Crossing road** — *"whoever needed somewhere the aligned powers don't reach."* **A bare
bearing cannot say "as far from that road as the town allows." A named road with `away` can, and it stays
true if the road moves** — which matters, because SNG-407 moved eleven locations.

### §2a — Two placements I want to point at

⛔ **The Crossing→Axis Gate road is the only STRAIGHT road I have authored**, and deliberately: *twelve
roads leave the Crossing, one per axis.* **A road that bends is a road that favours**, and the entire
point of that ground is that it does not.

⚠️ **The road to the Quiet House bends to keep off nothing** — it is widened, sight-lined and
deliberately dull, because **enemies walk it in both directions and are not permitted to meet on it.**

## §3 — ⛔ TWO SCHEMA CHANGES THE AUTHORING FORCED

**1 · `level` IS NEEDED AT REGION SCALE, not just on sites.**
The Slow Stair is *"the long way up and out — DAYS of ascending stair."* ⛔ **Its level changes ALONG its
length** — `-3` → `-2` → `0` across three waypoints — **which no other route in the world does.** I
authored `level` for interiors; a whole region can be vertical. **Expect it on ways and on named ground.**

**2 · AN EDGE OF KIND `none`.**
The Palelands and the Quickwood are **antipodal dispositions and DO NOT TOUCH.** The Hollowing looks
across **226 days of world** at a green it will never reach. ⚠️ **Recorded explicitly so that nobody draws
a border there** — an absent edge is a fact, and a renderer left to infer will infer wrongly.

## §4 — WHAT I AM NOT SUPPLYING, RESTATED

⛔ **Not the ground.** You bake coastline and relief from the generator and it cannot disagree with the
world because it IS the world evaluated once. **I supply only what lies below the information floor:** the
bends in the ways, the named ground, the edges, and the one line a narrator needs when a player is
crossing rather than arriving.

⚠️ **Every way carries its bend and its reason.** The Crossing→Stilts route follows the Echo downstream
until the river gives up and becomes marsh — **the bend is the river's, not the road's.** A generated way
that cannot say why it bends is a straight line with noise on it.
