| kind | axis | who |
|---|---|---|
| **solid** | Light | **Lucifer** |

| **held by the savior side** | Breaking / Building | ⛑ **the Last Mercy**. Challenged by the Scouring Hand (Breaking) and Cinder Vael (Building) |

## §2 — WHY THESE TWO

**Neth** is the lore's own first example in *"HELD, NOT OPPOSED"*. She has buried more than she has known and never
stopped attending, so she refuses neither the dying nor the living. She is already Morvane's counterweight.

**The Last Mercy** is the Scouring Hand's counterweight. She unmakes only what should end, and then stops. Restraint
in *breaking* is rarer than restraint in building, which is why she is the holder rather than Halcyon. (Halcyon, *Who
Built One Perfect Thing*, is her natural ally against Cinder Vael, the Wright Who Would Not Stop.)

⚑ **Both holders are epic and all four challengers are legendary.** That is deliberate, and the lore says why:
*"restraint is thinner, and it holds anyway."* A holder is in the way; she is not stronger than the thing trying to
get past her. **So a held seat is where the player matters most.** Defending the holder is the whole war for that
axis.

⚑ **What each holder knows:** what she is holding back, but not that it is a seat. The counterweights *"mostly do not
know"*, and a holder is the counterweight who stands in one place.

## §3 — ⬜ C18, FOR CCODE

A claimant finishing, which the lore calls *"a villain you fail to stop is a promotion"*, **cannot promote them into a
held seat while the holder stands.** If the holder is slain, turned or broken, the seat opens, and the challenger who
did it may finish into it. The open seat (Chaos / Order) is promoted by a finish alone. Holder, challengers and state
live on the save, GM-only.
