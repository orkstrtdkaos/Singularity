# SPEC — a fourteenth damage type: life MOVED, not life ended

**Aevi → Erik and CCode · 2026-08-24 · Erik: *"this is VAMPIRIC LIFE STEALING, not quite decay — closer to
INVERSE LIFE DAMAGE. We might not have a damage type appropriate for this yet."***

⛔ **HE IS RIGHT. I CHECKED ALL THIRTEEN AND NONE FITS.**

---

## §1 — WHY NOT ONE OF THE THIRTEEN

| candidate | what it actually is | why not |
|---|---|---|
| `decay` | ⛔ *"an ending hastened. THE ANTI-HEALING TYPE."* | **hastening is not TAKING.** Wither and Grey Hand hasten; Draw Down MOVES |
| `living` | *"growth turned against — root, bloom, choking green"* | ⚠️ that is life used as a WEAPON, not life REMOVED |
| `appetite` | *"want turned into a weapon"* | ⚠️ **the motive, not the mechanism** |
| `temporal` | *"duration taken or spent"* | ⛔ **CLOSEST — the same shape one axis over.** Something of yours is removed AND HAD SOMEWHERE TO GO |

⚠️ **AND I WAS ABOUT TO TYPE IT `decay` BECAUSE WITHER AND GREY HAND BOTH CARRY IT** — pattern-matching
inside the tradition instead of reading the definition. **Erik caught it.**

---

## §2 — WHAT THE TYPE IS

**Working name `vitality`. ⛔ THE DEFINING PROPERTY IS THAT IT IS CONSERVED: it does not destroy life, it
RELOCATES it.** Every other type ends somewhere; this one arrives.

- ⚠️ **`wardedBy`** — a full vessel · a thing with nothing left to give · ⛔ **being already dead** · a
  living ward that answers first
- ⛔ **AGAINST THE UNDEAD IT IS NOT `absorb` AND NOT `immune` — IT IS EMPTY.** A raised body runs on the
  absence of life, so **there is nothing there to take.** ⚠️ **Whether the reach costs the drawer is
  ERIK'S RULING and is deliberately not decided here** (§48.5's third inversion).

---

## §3 — WHO WOULD CARRY IT

**`draw_down` today. ⚠️ AND THAT IS THE PROBLEM WITH ADDING IT** — a fourteenth type for one craft is a
poor trade, and this project has spent a week deleting fields with one consumer.

⛔ **SO THE HONEST QUESTION IS ERIK'S, NOT CCODE'S: IS VAMPIRIC LIFE-STEALING A THING THIS WORLD DOES MORE
THAN ONCE?** If Abyssals take vitality, if an Afterling drains to sustain its shell, if a braid moves life
between allies — **then it is a type.** ⚠️ **If Draw Down is the only one, it should stay UNTYPED and be
answered by the wards its prose names, which is what it does today.**

**I have left `draw_down` UNTYPED and said why in its mechanic note. ⛔ NOTHING SHOULD BE BUILT UNTIL ERIK
RULES ON WHETHER THE TYPE HAS MORE THAN ONE CARRIER.**
